### How to Run an MTD IT Report

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 27 Mar, 2025 at  3:37 PM

Navigation: Self Assessment > MTD IT Income Report

Once in this section, chose the Tax Year in the drop down available and click 'Download Report'.

Once downloaded, you should be able to find an Excel Workbook in your download folder. It will look similar to the one below:

Evident in the file, you can see which of your clients are applicable for April 2026, and further which clients would be applicable in April 2027.
