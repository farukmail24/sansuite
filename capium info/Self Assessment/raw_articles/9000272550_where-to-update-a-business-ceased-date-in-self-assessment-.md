### Where to Update a Business Ceased Date in Self Assessment?

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 27 Nov, 2025 at  2:47 PM

		To update the cessation date of your business, please navigate to the Self Employment page >> Business Details >> Box 7 (Full form)/Box 6 (Short form): Date business ceased >> Update the details >> Save.

Note: If you wish to provide any additional information, please refer to the Main Form >> Finishing your tax return >> Your tax advisor >> Box no 19: Any other information.
