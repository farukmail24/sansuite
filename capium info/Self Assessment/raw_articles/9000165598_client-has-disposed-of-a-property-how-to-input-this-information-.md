### Client has disposed of a property. How to input this information?

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 25 Mar, 2019 at  1:54 PM

		You need to input the value of any gains made on the disposal of the interest in residential properties and will be the gain minus any relief, claim or election. 

If you want to claim the complete amount of gain as a relief, then just input code PPR or let in box 8 and leave box 6 blank. 

As a result, it would not reflect under the capital gain tax summary in the SA302 computation.
