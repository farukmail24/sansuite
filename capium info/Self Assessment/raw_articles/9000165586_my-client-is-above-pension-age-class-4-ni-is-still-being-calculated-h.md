### My client is above pension age; class 4 NI is still being calculated. How to remove this NI amount?

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 25 Mar, 2019 at  1:44 PM

		Box 26 "you are exempt from class 4 NIC?" under partnership (short)>>trading or professional losses>>NICs & taxed interest has not been selected, hence the class 4 NIC is being calculated. 
You may select Box 26 to claim the class 4 NIC.
