### SA100 Dashboard

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 1 Mar, 2024 at  3:50 PM

### Location: Self Assessment > SA100 > Client Specific > Dashboard

### 

### Alternatively, you may click on this link to be redirected to the page

### 

### Select the client for who Personal Tax return is to be filled from the client drop-down list from the Capium Dashboard. It could be filled for two types of clients:

### Individual clients    

### Sole Trader clients

### 

### Once you select the client you can see the client’s dashboard which has 3 sections:

Client Details: this shows the basic details of the client, you can also edit the client details

Tax Summary: this shows the basic taxable and tax payable details

SA100 Forms: this shows SA100 returns that have been generated, you can also add new SA100 from here.
