Trust Tax return is a self-assessment tax for Trusts. Please note that you may file a SA900 only when you have created a client as Trust type. 

### Capium Self-assessment module helps in delivering high-quality Tax work for a wide range of clients at highly efficient cost. 

### Its unique features simplify and streamline every step in the tax return process, from collecting raw data to online filing and tax planning.

Brief Intro:
A trust is a way of managing assets (money, investments, land or buildings) for people. There are different types of trusts and they are taxed differently.
Trusts involve:    

 - 
### the ‘settlor’ - the person who puts assets into a trust   

 - 
### the ‘trustee’ - the person who manages the trust  

 - 
### the ‘beneficiary’ - the person who benefits from the trust

### 
Capium SA900 module supports various types of Trust for whom the return can be filed:    

 - 
### Estate    

 - 
### Bare trust   

 - 
### Interest in possession Trust   

 - 
### Discretionary Trust   

 - 
### Charitable Trust  

 - 
### Unauthorized unit Trust   

 - 
### Employment-related Trust

 - 
### Heritage maintenance Fund   

 -
