### How to Claim Rent a Room Relief in Self Assessment ?

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 27 Nov, 2025 at  2:48 PM

		If you are receiving the error message ""There is an entry in box [PRO37], box [PRO4] must be absent. Please amend the incorrect entry." 

You may update the Total rent and other income from the property under box 20 and you may please tick box 4 to claim rent-a-room relief. If the total income is less than the threshold you may tick Box 4 and not necessary to update the rest of the boxes. If the total income is more than the threshold you may do either of  

• pay tax on the excess – without taking off any expenses – put your total income in box 20 and the exempt amount of £7,500 (or £3,750) in box 37 – you cannot claim the property income allowance on Rent a Room income 

• work out your profit from letting in the usual way – the amount in box 20 minus any allowable expenses in boxes 24 to 29 – do not put anything in box 37. 

Please refer to the attached HMRC guidance notes for more information.
