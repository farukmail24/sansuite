### Overview

The Tax Summary section provides an overview of the taxpayer's tax position for a selected tax year. It displays key tax figures, including taxable income, tax charged, and payment amounts due.

### Navigation

Self Assessment > SA100 > Client Specific > Tax Summary

### Review the Tax Summary

The Tax Summary displays the following information for the selected year:

 - Total Taxable Income

 - Total Tax Charged

 - Total Payment Due by 31 January

 - Total Payment Due by 31 July

Note: The payment due dates displayed will correspond to the selected tax year.
