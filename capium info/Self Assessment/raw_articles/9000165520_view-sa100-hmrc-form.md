### View SA100 HMRC Form

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 30 Jun, 2026 at  5:52 PM

### Overview

The View Form feature allows you to review the completed SA100 return in the official HMRC format. You can navigate through the main return and any attached supplementary forms, as well as download a PDF copy for review or record-keeping.

### Navigation

Self Assessment > SA100 > Client Specific > Reports > SA100 (All Forms)

### Review the HMRC Forms and Supplementary pages 

The return will open in the official HMRC format, displaying all the information entered for the tax return.

If supplementary forms have been added to the return, they will be displayed as tabs at the top of the page.

Select a tab to review the information entered for each supplementary form.

You will also be able to view:

- The SA302 computation

- Payment on Account 

- Detailed Schedule

### To Download or Return:

Use the Download PDF button in the top-right corner to save a copy of the SA100 return.
