### There is a delay in receiving the Acknowledgement from HMRC on submitting SA return.

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 25 Mar, 2019 at  1:51 PM

		The delay relating to polling of responses is from HMRC's end only. 

Capium only acts as a medium of submission and the response is generated from HMRC's end only. It generally takes 10-15 seconds and then you can poll the HMRC response using the "action" dropdown there. 

However, at certain times of high traffic volume to their servers, this issue can arise.
