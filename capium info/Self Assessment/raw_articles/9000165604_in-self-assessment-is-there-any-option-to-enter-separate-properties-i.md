### In Self-Assessment is there any option to enter separate properties individually?

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 25 Mar, 2019 at  2:00 PM

		As of now, there is no such option to enter separate properties individually. You may tick the box "property let jointly" even if one of the several properties are let so.
