### How Can I Add Voluntary Class 2 NIC in Self Assessment?

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 27 Nov, 2025 at  2:48 PM

		Please adhere to the following steps for the addition of Voluntary Class 2 National Insurance Contributions.

1. Deselect the Auto Calculate Class 2 NIC checkbox located under "Add Supplementary form." 

2. After completing this step, you may input the desired values by clicking the Green '+' sign under the Tax Calculation tab.

Please refer to the help guide below regarding the process for adding voluntary Class 2 National Insurance Contributions for the client, as well as the calculations of NIC in accordance with HMRC guidelines.
