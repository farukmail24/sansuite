			Print

###   

### 

The Main Form covers 3 sections;

 - 
### Income    

 - 
### Tax reliefs         

 - 
### Finishing your tax returns

### 

### 

### 1- Income

### This section contains income from different sources which are to be mentioned while filing a SA100 return. The details regarding following Source of Income can be mentioned:

 - 
### Interest and Dividends from UK banks, Building Societies  

 - 
### UK Pensions, Annuities, and other Benefits Received  

 - 
### Other UK income not included on Supplementary Pages

Each section under different tabs are clickable that will allow you to navigate to the data entry part with respect to each listed section.

One can click on a particular section to open as a Pop-Up, where necessary details regarding Incomes have to be filled in the Relevant Tabs.

‘+’ button next to any text box will allow an Assessee to feed multiple entries with regards to different incomes and other details in a bifurcated manner for their understanding. 

Best example where such entries are beneficial could be Bank interest, dividends, other incomes etc. where its necessary to maintain multiple rows of a particular transaction covering up different sources. But on the main form, it will show as a single amount.  

### 

### 2 - Tax Reliefs and Service Companies

This Section includes:       

 - 
### The Pension Payment        

 - 
### Charitable Giving       

 - 
### Student Loan Repayments and Blind Person’s Allowance      

 - 
### High Income Child  Benefit Charge and Service Companies

### You may click on the relevant hyperlinks there, which will open a Pop-Up where you may mention details regarding Income under Relevant Tabs.

### 

3-Finishing Your Tax Return

### 

### It the final Section which includes the following:

 - 
### Tax Refunded or set off   

 - 
### If you have paid too much tax    

 - 
### Your tax adviser if you have one   

 - 
### Signing your form and sending it  back

###
