### How to automatically pull employment information from HMRC (Self Assessment)

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 22 Apr, 2024 at 12:18 PM

		Location: 

Select client > HMRC Authorisation > authorise Capium with HMRC > create new return > within the return navigate to 'HMRC connect' > click on 'Populate employment data' 

Video Tutorial:

Help Guide: 

The first step is to authorise Capium with HMRC. This will connect the two portals and allow you to pull the employment information into Capium. Navigate into the clients dashboard > click on 'HMRC authorisation' > Click on 'HMRC authentication' 

Proceed to create an SA100 as normal.

There is now  a new tab called 'HMRC connect'. This will show all employment income information that has been pulled from HMRC. To continue, click on 'Populate employment data'. 

Capium will then proceed to create the employment supplementary pages with all of the information entered in.
