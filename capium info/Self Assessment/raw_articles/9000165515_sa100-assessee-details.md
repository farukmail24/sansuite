### SA100 Assessee Details

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 30 Jun, 2026 at  5:38 PM

### Overview

The Assessee Details section contains the taxpayer's personal and HMRC information used when preparing an SA100 Tax Return. It is important to ensure these details are accurate before submitting the return, as certain fields are mandatory and some cannot be amended after submission to HMRC.

### Navigation

Personal Tax > Select Client > SA100 Return > Assessee Details

### 

### Step 1: Add Assessee Details

The Assessee Details section displays the following information:

 - Owner Name (Sole Traders only)

 - Tax Reference Number (UTR)

 - Registered In (England and Wales or Scotland) (Mandatory)

 - Address

 - Postcode

 - Date of Birth (Mandatory)

 - Phone Number

 - National Insurance Number (Mandatory)

 - HMRC Office Details

 - Assign as Director

Note: The Tax Reference Number (UTR) is mandatory and cannot be changed after the return has been submitted to HMRC.

### Step 2: Save Your Changes

After updating the information, save your changes to apply them to the SA100 return.
