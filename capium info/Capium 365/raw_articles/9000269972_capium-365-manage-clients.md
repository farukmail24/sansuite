### Capium 365: Manage Clients

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 8 Oct, 2025 at  6:58 PM

Add new clients to Capium 365. 
Edit and Delete existing clients in Capium 365 
Log into a client’s Capium 365 portal.   
Search and filter clients to quickly locate records.
