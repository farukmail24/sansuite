### What I can I import from the bookkeeping module into Capium 365?

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 13 Dec, 2024 at  4:30 PM

		You can import: customers, suppliers, Products, Bank Feeds, Bank Statements, Account Names, VAT Rates and Users.

You have to import these individually for each client. To log onto the client portal > Click the settings cog in the top right corner > Scroll down to imports > Click this. Here you will find the different imports for the client.
