### Capium 365: How to add Customers invoices

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 29 Jul, 2025 at 11:46 AM

		Below is the step- by- step Guide to add customers into the capium 365

Navigation: Capium 365 > Client Portal > Invoices >Customers

Customers

When you click on Invoices, you’ll be taken to the main Invoices page. From here, you can select Customers, upon selecting customers 

Adding Customers:

First click onto '+add Customers' in there 

Upon clicking on Add Customers

Once you've selected or added a customer, please ensure all relevant information is filled in accurately:

Customer ID

Account Name

Payment Account

VAT Rate

Contact Details 

Address Details 

After entering all the required details, click Save to update the customer profile.

By this you can add customer into Capium 365
