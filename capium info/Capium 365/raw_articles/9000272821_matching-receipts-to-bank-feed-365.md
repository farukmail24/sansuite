### Matching receipts to bank feed -365

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 10 Dec, 2025 at  3:37 PM

		To align receipts with the bank feed, the user must first record the payment for the bill; only then will the transaction be available for reconciliation.

Navigation: 365>>Client>>Purchases>>Bill 

After editing the bill, please make sure to record the payment as shown below. Once the payment is recorded, the transaction will be available for reconciliation.
