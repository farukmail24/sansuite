### How do I invite clients to Capium 365?

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 1 Apr, 2025 at 11:13 AM

		To access the clients workspace and for the client to be able to access the end user app, they first must be invited into Capium 365 and accept this invitation.

To invite a client into Capium 365: Ensure you have enough licences (You can check this on the Dashboard in the client portal) > Go to "Manage" > Click the "Clients" tab > Find the client you want to invite > Click "Invite" under 365 status > An email will then be sent to your client where they can accept the invitation > Once the client has accepted the invitation the 365 status will change to "Active"
