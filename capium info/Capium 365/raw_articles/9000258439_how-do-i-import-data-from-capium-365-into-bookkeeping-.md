### How do I import data from Capium 365 into Bookkeeping?

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 11 Dec, 2024 at  4:48 PM

		There are several integrations between the existing bookkeeping module and Capium 365.

If you go to client portal > settings icon in the top right corner > Click this > Imports > Here you can see that there are several options for importing from bookkeeping > Click what you want to import and follow the process.
