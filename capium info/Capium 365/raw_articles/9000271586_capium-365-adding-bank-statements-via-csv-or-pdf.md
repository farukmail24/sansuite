### CAPIUM 365: Adding Bank Statements Via CSV or PDF

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 9 Oct, 2025 at 10:20 AM

		Adding Bank Statements Via CSV or PDF

You can upload statements in bulk if you prefer not to use a bank feed.

Navigation:Capium 365 > Client > Settings (top right) > Imports > Bank Statements

Steps:

Click the Settings (cog icon) in the top right of Capium.

Select "Imports".

Choose "Bank Statements" and click Next.

Select the format you want to upload (PDF or Excel/CSV).

Upload the statement file into Capium.

Note: If you upload via CSV you can download a template file and upload it back into Capium.
