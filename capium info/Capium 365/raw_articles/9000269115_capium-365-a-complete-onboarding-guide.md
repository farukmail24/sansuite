### Capium 365: A Complete Onboarding Guide

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 26 Jun, 2025 at  4:32 PM

		Capium 365: A Complete Onboarding Guide

This article provides a full overview of how to get started with Capium 365. The onboarding process has been broken down into a series of key steps, with each stage supported by its own dedicated guide.

1. How to purchase a Capium 365 license
Learn how to access your subscription settings, select your plan, and complete the payment process.

Click on the link below to learn more about how to purchase a 365 license: https://capium.freshdesk.com/a/solutions/articles/9000246860?portalId=9000044273

2. Assigning a Capium 365 license to your client

After purchasing, you’ll need to assign the license to a specific client (individual or sole trader) from within Capium.

Click on the link below to learn more about how to assign a 365 license to your client:

https://capium.freshdesk.com/a/solutions/articles/9000268736?portalId=9000044273

3. Inviting clients to join Capium 365

Send an invitation to your client so they can access their Capium 365 portal and collaborate with you directly.

Click on the link below to learn more about inviting clients to join Capium 365:

https://capium.freshdesk.com/a/solutions/articles/9000269004?portalId=9000044273 

4. Syncing Capium 365 with the Bookkeeping module

Link your client’s bank accounts to Capium 365 and set up rules to automatically categorise transactions.

https://capium.freshdesk.com/a/solutions/articles/9000269006?portalId=9000044273

6. Adding receipts in Capium 365

Upload and manage receipts through manual entry or by using image capture options.

https://capium.freshdesk.com/a/solutions/articles/9000269008?portalId=9000044273

Log payments and match them to relevant invoices, suppliers, or clients.

Take advantage of Capium 365’s mobile app to manage receipts, review transactions, and communicate with clients on the go.
