### How to delete Bank Accounts and Bank Feeds in Capium 365

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 3 Jun, 2026 at  1:28 PM

		Bank Accounts and Bank Feeds cannot be deleted directly within Capium 365.

To request a deletion, please raise a support ticket and provide:

 - The client name
 - The bank account(s) and/or bank feed(s) you would like removed
Our team will process the deletion from the backend and confirm once it has been completed.
