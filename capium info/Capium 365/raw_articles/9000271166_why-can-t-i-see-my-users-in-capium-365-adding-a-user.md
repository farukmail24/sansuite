### Why can&#39;t I see my users in Capium 365? - Adding a User

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 17 Sep, 2025 at 10:44 AM

		Capium 365 - Users

Navigation: Capium Home > 'C' icon > Capium 365 > Manage

Users will appear in Manage > Users once they’ve accessed the Capium 365 module. To do this, they just need to click the ‘C’ icon at the top and open the Capium 365 module at least once. After that, they will automatically be added to the Users list.
