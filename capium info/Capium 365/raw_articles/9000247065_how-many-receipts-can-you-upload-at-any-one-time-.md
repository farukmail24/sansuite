### How many receipts can you upload at any one time?

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 1 Apr, 2025 at 12:56 PM

		You can upload up to 50 receipts (Files) at any one time. However, as soon as you have uploaded 50 receipts in one batch, you can upload another batch of 50 receipts. You can upload in the formats: JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP, AVIF, HEIF, WEBP, and PDF.
