### CAPIUM 365: Adding and Managing Transactions

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 15 Oct, 2025 at  5:22 PM

		This guide explains how to add and manage transactions in the Books section of Capium 365, whether manually, via bank feeds, or by importing statements.

Note: Books section is only available for individuals and sole traders who are not registered for VAT. For customers above the VAT threshold or other entities (i.e. Limited Companies and Partnerships) the software will post the invoices and purchases directly to the Bookkeeping module. 

Navigation: Capium 365 Module > Client > Books

This section displays transactions from multiple bank banks under the books section, which makes it easier for the accountants to classify the information based on account name and source.  

Adding a New Transaction

New Transactions can be added in three ways:

Adding transactions one at a time
Bank Feeds Import 
Bank statement import 
Adding Transactions one at a time

Navigation: Capium 365 > Client > Books > + Transaction

1. Go to Client > Books.

2. Click on the "+ Transaction" button.

3. Fill in the transaction details:

Transaction Type: Choose between Income or Expense.
Supplier: Select or add a supplier.
Date: Set the transaction date. (Mandatory Field) 
Transaction Amount: Enter the amount for the transaction. (Mandatory Field) 
Payment Account: Select the relevant payment account or create a new account (Mandatory Field). Here you can only add transactions based on the bank entries. 
Payment Status: Mark as paid (if applicable).
Account Name: Assign the transaction to a specific account.
Reference: Enter a unique reference if needed.
Source: Select the transaction source.
4. Save and Exit

Bringing in transactions with a Bank Feed

Connect your bank accounts directly to Capium to automatically pull transactions into the Books section. Bank feeds are free and unlimited with Capium 365.

Bank Feed Rules:

Bank feeds will be fetched starting from the client’s invite or activation date. All transactions before that date must be reconciled in Bookkeeping if the client exists there and was imported from MyAdmin.

If you need any older transactions in 365 (prior to the invite date), navigate to Settings → Import → Bank Statements → Bank Import.
Please note that Bookkeeping reconciliation status is not displayed in 365, so ensure that the same transaction is not reconciled in both systems.

For clients created directly in 365, the past 90 days of transactions will be available for reconciliation.

  Navigation: Capium 365 > Client > Bank Feeds > 

Steps:

Go to Client > Bank Feeds.

Click "+ Add New Account".

Select your banking provider from the list.

Click "Allow" and follow the prompts to connect your account.

Once connected, your bank transactions will start appearing automatically.

Adding Bank Statements Via CSV or PDF

You can upload statements in bulk if you prefer not to use a bank feed.

Navigation:Capium 365 > Client > Settings (top right) > Imports > Bank Statements

Steps:

Click the Settings (cog icon) in the top right of Capium.

Select "Imports".

Choose "Bank Statements" and click Next.

Select the format you want to upload (PDF or Excel/CSV).

Upload the statement file into Capium.

Note: If you upload via CSV you can download a template file and upload it back into Capium.

### Editing Transactions

Use this option to make changes to individual transactions after they’ve been added.

Navigation: Capium 365 > Client > Books

Steps:

Go to Client > Books.

Find the transaction you want to edit.

Click the pencil icon on the right-hand side.

Make the necessary changes.

Click "Save and Exit".

Bulk Editing Transactions

Edit multiple transactions at once by changing the account name or source.

Navigation: Capium 365 > Client > Books

Steps:

Go to Client > Books.

Tick the boxes beside each transaction you want to edit.

Or click the checkbox in the top-left to select all.

Click "Bulk Edit".

Update the Source and/or Account Name as needed.

Click "Save".

### Managing Transactions

There are several ways where a user can manage the transactions in the books section.

Categorisation (Allocating nominal accounts and sources to transactions) 
Adding CIS transactions
Creating queries and reviewing them (Sole trader +individual)
Attaching Invoices or Scans to payments
Matching transactions to purchases and sales
