### How can I purchase licences in Capium 365?

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 16 May, 2025 at  2:44 PM

		You can purchase licences in Capium 365 in one of two ways:

1. You can call us on 02033225578 and speak to one of the sales team directly

2. Login in to Capium > MyAdmin > Click “MySubscription” at the top > Click “Capium 365” > Select how many clients you want to purchase > Click “Go To Cart” > Click “Upgrade Plan”
