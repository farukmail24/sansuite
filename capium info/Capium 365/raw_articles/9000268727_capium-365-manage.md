### Capium 365: Manage

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 8 Oct, 2025 at  6:56 PM

		The Capium 365 Manage section provides accountants with a centralised hub to oversee all their clients efficiently. It offers a high-level, bird’s-eye view across multiple key areas, streamlining practice-wide operations. 

Key management areas include: 

Clients – Access and manage your entire client portfolio from a single interface. 

Users – Add/remove staff users. 

Imports – Seamlessly import data to reduce manual entry. 

Permissions – Manage team access and roles across the platform.
