### What is Capium 365 and how does it help accountants?

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 28 Jan, 2026 at 12:23 PM

		Capium 365 is a client portal and mobile app that helps clients upload receipts/invoices, connect bank feeds, and share documents. Accountants can review, approve and (where applicable) publish data into Bookkeeping, supporting better month-to-month records and—where relevant—MTD IT quarterly submissions.
