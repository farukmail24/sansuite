### Capium 365 - Receipts (App)

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 23 Apr, 2025 at 10:31 AM

		Welcome to our Guide for Receipts in Capium 365! This guide will walk you through essential initial steps, best practices and key features within Suppliers.

How to create a supplier

Click the three dashes in the top left-hand corner of the screen
Navigate to “Receipts” and click this to open the drop down menu
Select the “Suppliers” icon in the left panel
Select the “+” in the bottom right hand corner
Populate the supplier details as required
Select “Save”

How to add receipts

Click the three dashes in the top left-hand corner of the screen
Navigate to the “Receipts” tab
Select the “+” in the bottom right of the screen
Select either the camera icon OR the gallery icon
Capium allows you to either take a photo of the receipt which is the camera icon in the middle of the screen, or choose an existing photo from your gallery. 

You can add up to 50 receipts at a time. Once a receipt has been added, there are five different statuses:

Processing – The system is uploading the receipts
Review – Once a receipt has been processed and is awaiting approval
Approved – If the accountant has approved the receipt
Rejected – If the accountant rejects the receipt
Archived – Receipts are deleted 30 days after when they are marked as archive

You can export the receipts. To do this, you need to:

Click the three dashes in the top left-hand corner of the screen
Navigate to the “Receipts” tab
Click on “receipts” on the drop-down menu
Click on the 3 dots in the top right-hand corner
You have to option to export to Excel, CSV and PDF

How to approve, reject, archive, delete and reject receipts 

Click the three dashes in the top left-hand corner of the screen
Navigate to the “Receipts” tab
Click on “receipts” on the drop-down menu
Press down on the receipt until it turns purple
Click on the three dots in the top right hand corner of the screen
A drop-down menu should appear with several options, click the option you want

How to edit receipts 

Click the three dashes in the top left-hand corner of the screen
Navigate to the “Receipts” tab
Click on “receipts” on the drop-down menu
You can only edit receipts which are in the review state
Click on a receipt which is in the review state
Edit the receipt
Scroll down and press save
