### Capium 365: Setting up bank feed

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 28 Jul, 2025 at  2:29 PM

### Connecting your bank account to Capium 365 allows for seamless transaction imports and makes reconciliation quick and accurate. Follow the steps below to set up your bank feed from your client login

### Log in to Capium 365

Visit: https://365client.capium.com

Enter your email and password, then click Log in

After successful login, you will be redirected to the dashboard:https://365client.capium.com/dashboard

### Navigate to the Bank Section

On the dashboard, go to Navigation > Bank > Add New Account

If you don’t have a bank account listed yet, add one here before connecting the feed

Please refer the screenshot below:

### Choose Your Bank

You’ll be redirected to a secure page listing various UK banks

Search and select your bank from the list

### Authorise the Connection

You’ll now be redirected to your bank’s secure login portal

Log in using your online banking credentials

Select the account(s) you wish to connect

Give consent to share the data with Capium (this is a standard Open Banking authorisation)

Click Confirm/Allow to finish

Please refer the screenshot below:

Note: Sync Your Transactions: Once authorised, Capium will begin importing transactions Automaticlly. 

You can go to Bank > Transactions to view and reconcile

If you're stuck or have issues connecting your bank, feel free to: 

Use the support option within Capium 365 to raise a ticket
