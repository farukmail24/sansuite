### MTD IT: Quarterly Submissions and the Final End of Year Declaration

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 6 Oct, 2025 at  3:01 PM

		With Capium’s new MTD Income Tax (MTD IT) module, you will be able to manage both quarterly updates and the final end of year declaration. This will be all within one streamlined process saving you time and money.

1. Overview of the MTD IT Module

Capium is in the final stages of developing its dedicated MTD IT module. This new feature has already been successfully tested with multiple quarterly submissions to HMRC. The final user journey is currently being refined to make the process as simple and efficient as possible for users.

2. Submitting Quarterly Updates

Once available, the MTD IT module will allow you to:

Submit your quarterly updates directly to HMRC.
The MTD IT module can be used as an independent bridging solution. This will work very similar to how you submit bridging VAT in bookkeeping.
Review and confirm submission summaries before filing.
Use the same familiar Capium interface you already know.

3. Submitting the Final End of Year Declaration

In addition to quarterly updates, the MTD IT module will also enable you to:

Complete and submit your final declaration to HMRC.
Ensure that all income and adjustments for the year are included.

4. Capium 365: An Integrated Bookkeeping Tool

For users seeking a full bookkeeping and MTD solution, Capium also offers Capium 365, which includes:

Unlimited receipt and invoice scanning
Unlimited bank feeds
End user app 
Automatic bank reconciliation
End-user mobile app for easy access and uploads

Capium 365 is designed to save you time and money and will automate the bookkeeping process. With Capium 365, you’ll also receive free quarterly and end-of-year submissions, giving you a complete, integrated approach to MTD compliance.
