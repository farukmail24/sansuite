### What does the "Requests" tab mean in the Client Portal in Capium 365?

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 24 Oct, 2024 at  3:33 PM

		If you require certain information from your client, you can create requests asking for certain information or documents from your client. You have the option to send these to as many or as little clients as possible. The end user will be notified by email and their app, where they can respond to the information request.

You can also create request templates in the manage section of the client portal which allow you to quickly put together a document request.
