### How to log in to Capium 365? (Mobile)

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 25 Jul, 2025 at  4:59 PM

		The Capium 365 mobile app allows clients to access their accounting data, scan receipts, and share documents directly from their smartphone. 

Logging into Capium 365 

After installation, follow these steps to sign in: 

Launch the Capium 365 App from your home screen. 

Enter your Email ID, Capium will always send you an OTP whenever you are signing in on a new device or whenever a session times out. 

and Check your email for a 6-digit verification code. 

Enter the verification code in the app to verify your email address. 

Please find the below screenshots:

You will need to set a PIN which will allow you to sign in to your app whenever you open it. If you forget your pin you will be able to reset that PIN. Please click here to be redirected to the reset PIN page. 

Once logged in you would be redirected to the page below where you are able to scan receipts, scan invoice, link your bank account and access your dashboards.
