### CAPIUM 365: Bringing in transactions with a Bank Feed

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 9 Oct, 2025 at 10:19 AM

		Connect your bank accounts directly to Capium to automatically pull transactions into the Books section. Bank feeds are free and unlimited with Capium 365.

Navigation: Capium 365 > Client > Bank Feeds > 

Steps:

Go to Client > Bank Feeds.

Click "+ Add New Account".

Select your banking provider from the list.

Click "Allow" and follow the prompts to connect your account.

Once connected, your bank transactions will start appearing automatically.
