### What happens to receipts when they are approved?

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 1 Apr, 2025 at 12:55 PM

		Once receipts are approved, they will display on expense reports. They can also be attached to transactions.

For limited companies and VAT registered clients the approved receipts can then be published into bookkeeping.
