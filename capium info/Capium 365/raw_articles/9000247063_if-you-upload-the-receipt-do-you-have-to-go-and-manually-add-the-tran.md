### If you upload the receipt do you have to go and manually add the transaction?

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 25 Oct, 2024 at  3:54 PM

		Yes, a receipt will not automatically create a transaction. You can attach receipts to transactions in books. Receipts with the same key information will also match to existing transactions. Transactions can be brought in through bank feeds. You only have to add transactions, if it was cash based or not included in the bank feeds.
