### How can I bulk edit transactions in books?

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 11 Dec, 2024 at  4:50 PM

		If you go to “Books” in the client portal, find the transactions you want to bulk edit, click on the box on the left hand side of the transaction, there is the option in the middle which is called "Bulk Edit". You can bulk edit the “Account Name” and the “Source” of the transaction.

Please note that you can only bulk edit transactions when they are unlocked and not published.
