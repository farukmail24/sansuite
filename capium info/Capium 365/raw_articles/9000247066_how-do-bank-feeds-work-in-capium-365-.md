### How do bank feeds work in Capium 365?

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 1 Apr, 2025 at  1:00 PM

		Truelayer is used to attach and bring in data from bank feeds. Truelayer supports most high street banks. You connect these in the bank feeds tab. All the transactions brought in through bank feeds will be shown in “Books”. These transactions are only editable for the accountant. In the edit section, you can add receipts, change the VAT rate, edit the source, categories the transaction, add/ remove CIS deductions.

When you invite a client into Capium 365 it will disconnect the bank feeds for that client in the regular bookkeeping module. You will then have to reconnect the bank feeds in Capium 365.

Bank feeds are free and unlimited in Capium 365. If you have the regular bookkeeping module in Capium 365, the bank feeds will also be free for the corresponding client in the Bookkeeping module.
