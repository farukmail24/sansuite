### Capium 365: How to Purchase Licenses

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 18 Jul, 2025 at 12:34 PM

Purchasing additional licenses for Capium 365 is quick and seamless. Follow the steps below to manage your subscription and ensure your team has the access they need.

Step-by-Step Guide

Navigation: My Admin > My Subscription > Capium 365
Alternatively please click on this link to be redirected. 

Access My Admin: From the Capium dashboard, navigate to the top-right corner and select My Admin.

Open My Subscription: Click on My Subscription to view your current subscription plan.

Choose Capium 365: Under the available options, select Capium 365.

Select License Quantity: Use the dropdown or input field to choose the number of additional licenses required.

Initiate Payment: Click Pay Now to proceed.

Enter Payment Details: Complete the payment form with your card details and billing information.
