### How do I download the information from returned Documents Requests?

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 24 Oct, 2024 at  4:56 PM

		If you go to: “Requests” > Click into a request which has been returned > Click the download icon in the right-hand corner, this will download all of the requests into individual folders with the client name and will compress it into one large zip file.
