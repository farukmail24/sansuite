### Search and filter clients to quickly locate records in Capium 365

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 22 Jul, 2025 at 10:12 AM

		Search and filter clients

Within the Capium 365 Manage dashboard, you can efficiently locate clients using the search bar, which allows searches by client ID, name, contact name, and more.

Additionally, the dashboard provides robust filtering options to help refine your view:

365 Status Filters:

All 365 Status
Invite
Active
Resend Invitation
Imported
Referred

General Status Filters:

All Status
Active
Inactive

These tools enable streamlined navigation and management of your client base within Capium 365.
