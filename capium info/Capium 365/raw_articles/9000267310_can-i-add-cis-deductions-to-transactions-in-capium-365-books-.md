### Can I add CIS deductions to transactions in Capium 365 Books?

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 1 Apr, 2025 at  3:18 PM

		Yes you can add CIS deductions directly into books for unregistered VAT clients. 

To do this go to: Capium 365 > “Books” > Find the income expenditure you want to add the CIS deduction to > Click the “+” Icon on the right hand side > Select the level of the CIS discount > Click “Save” > The CIS deduction will then be applied
