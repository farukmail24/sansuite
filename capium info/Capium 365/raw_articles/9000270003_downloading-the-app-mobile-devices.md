### Downloading the App mobile devices

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 25 Jul, 2025 at  4:59 PM

		The Capium 365 mobile app is built to give clients easy and secure access to their financial data—anytime, anywhere. Available on both Android and iOS devices, the app enables seamless collaboration between clients and their accountants, helping users stay connected,Scan and upload receipts or invoices instantly, Link your bank account for live transaction access .

### For iOS (iPhone/iPad) Users:

Open the App Store on your iPhone or iPad.

Use the search bar to look for "Capium 365".

Find the official app by Capium and tap "Get" to begin the download.

After the app is installed, open it and enter your login credentials from your Capium 365 invitation email.

Click on the link below to learn more about downloading and logging  in to the App (iOS) 

 https://capium.freshdesk.com/a/solutions/articles/9000267946?portalId=9000044273

###  For Android Users:

Open the Google Play Store on your Android phone or tablet.

In the search bar, type "Capium 365".

Locate the official Capium 365 app (published by Capium) and tap "Install".

Once installed, open the app and log in using the credentials provided in your invitation email.

If you haven’t accepted the invitation yet, make sure to complete that step via the link before logging in.

Click on the link below to learn more about downloading and logging in to the App (Android)

https://capium.freshdesk.com/a/solutions/articles/9000270002?portalId=9000044273
