### Capium 365: Manage Users

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 24 Jul, 2025 at  4:53 PM

		Click here to go to the 'Manage' page

Within the Capium 365 Manage section, the Users area serves as your central hub for efficient user management. This intuitive interface provides all the tools you need to maintain control over your team and their access. Here, you can: 

Add new users to your practice.  

Edit users to pdate user information to keep records accurate and up to date.

Assign users to specific clients to delegate responsibility. 

Search for users by name or status to quickly locate records.
