### Capium 365 - Bank Feeds (App)

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 22 Oct, 2024 at  2:39 PM

Welcome to our Guide for Bank Feeds in Capium 365! This guide will walk you through essential initial steps, best practices and key features within Bank Feeds.

How to link a bank account 

To begin using Capium 365’s Bank Feed feature, you will need to link a bank account. To do this, you need to: 

Click on the three dashes in the top left corner of the screen
Navigate to the “Bank Feeds” tab 
Click on the “+” icon in the bottom right-hand corner of the screen
Select the bank 
Select “Allow” 
Log in or populate the bank details as required 
Once completed, you will be taken back to Capium 365 and the bank account will be connected 

You can export bank transactions. To do this, you need to: 

Click on the three dashes in the top left corner of the screen
Navigate to the “Bank Feeds” tab 
Select the three dots in the right-hand corner 
Select between Excel, CSV or PDF Format 

You can refresh/resync bank transactions. To do this, you need to: 

Click on the three dashes in the top left corner of the screen
Navigate to the “Bank Feeds” Tab 
Select the refresh icon in the right hand side of the screen 

After every 90 days, the bank account will need to be reauthenticated. To do this, you need to: 

Click on the three dashes in the top left corner of the screen
Navigate to the “Bank Feeds” Tab 
Select “Reauthenticate” where you will be taken to TrueLayer 
Log in or populate the bank details as required 
Once completed, you will be taken back to Capium 365 and the bank account will be connected 

How to refresh a Bank Feed

Click on the three dashes in the top left corner of the screen
Navigate to the “Bank Feeds” Tab
Click the icon with the two arrows in the top right hand corner of the screen 

How to delete a bank account 

Click on the three dashes in the top left corner of the screen
Navigate to the “Bank Feeds” Tab  
Select the bin icon and press this
Select “Yes”
