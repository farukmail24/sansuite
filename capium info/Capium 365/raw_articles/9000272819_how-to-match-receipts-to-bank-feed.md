### How to Match receipts to bank feed

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 17 Dec, 2025 at 11:13 AM

		Matching receipts to bank feed: The client must record the payment for the bill only then will the transaction be visible for reconciliation.

Navigation : 365>>Client>>Purchases>>Bill 

Edit the Bill once done record the payment as shown below. As Payment is recorded transaction will be visible for reconciliation.
