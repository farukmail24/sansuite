### CAPIUM 365: Adding Transactions one at a time

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 9 Oct, 2025 at 10:18 AM

		Adding Transactions one at a time

Navigation: Capium 365 > Client > Books > + Transaction

1. Go to Client > Books.

2. Click on the "+ Transaction" button.

3. Fill in the transaction details:

Transaction Type: Choose between Income or Expense.
Supplier: Select or add a supplier.
Date: Set the transaction date. (Mandatory Field) 
Transaction Amount: Enter the amount for the transaction. (Mandatory Field) 
Payment Account: Select the relevant payment account or create a new account (Mandatory Field). Here you can only add transactions based on the bank entries. 
Payment Status: Mark as paid (if applicable).
Account Name: Assign the transaction to a specific account.
Reference: Enter a unique reference if needed.
Source: Select the transaction source.
4. Save and Exit
