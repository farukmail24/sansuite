### Schedule - Minutes of Meeting Notes

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 23 Aug, 2021 at  1:14 PM

		Location:  Bookkeeping module > Dashboard > Client specific > Schedule > Minutes of Meeting

Help/Guide  

Minutes of the meeting is a written or recorded documentation to inform attendees and non-attendees of the happenings during the meeting.

On clicking +Add page appears for new.

Minutes Include;

Writers Meeting

Meeting called to order at......by.......

Meeting Location

Meeting Type

Members Present

Reading of Agenda

Approval of Minutes

Business

Meeting adjourned at

Schedule Notes

You can add a note by +Note

You need to enter title and description.

Same would be listed on note page, You can also filter by title and description, and search the note.

For each note, you can select action as Edit Delete.
