### Contacts - Shareholders

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 22 May, 2020 at  1:26 PM

		Location:  Bookkeeping module > Dashboard > Client specific > Contacts > Shareholders

Click here to watch the video

This page will show you;

Name of the Shareholder.

Share type is Equity.

No. of shares.

Phone no# and Email ID and Country is displayed.

Action drop-down has an option to;

Make Director,

Edit,

Delete

You can export as a PDF by using the “Export\/” button on the top right of the page.

On clicking “+Shareholder”, can add new Shareholder, enter all the details. As seen in the image to the left.

Checkbox for active to be selected.

Enter no. of shares and value per share.

Select share type and save.
