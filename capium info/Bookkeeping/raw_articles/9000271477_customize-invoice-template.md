### Customize invoice template

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 22 Oct, 2025 at  7:13 AM

		Title: Using the Doc (Word) Invoice Template

We offer the option to use a Doc (Word) Invoice Template for your convenience. Here's how you can enable and use this feature:

1. Navigate to Bookkeeping >> Client specific >> Settings >> Company info >> Settings(tab).

2. Check the box for Use Doc (Word) Invoice Template.

3. Select Save to apply the changes.

Once you have completed these steps, you can proceed to the Invoice templates section to download the template. After downloading the template, you can make any necessary modifications to customize it to your needs. Once you have made the changes, you can upload the revised template to start using it for your invoices.

If you need further assistance with this process, please refer to the attached document for a visual guide. Our team is also available to provide support and answer any questions you may have.

We hope that this feature will make it easier for you to create and customize your invoices. If you have any feedback or suggestions for improvement, please feel free to reach out to us. We are committed to providing the best possible experience for our customers, and your input is valuable to us.
