### Retrieving Historical Bank Transactions via Bank Feeds

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 22 Oct, 2025 at  2:15 PM

Bank Feeds: How to Retrieve Previous Bank Transactions

After reconnecting bank feeds, you can retrieve the previous 90 days of bank transactions, depending on the bank. Here's how you can do it:

1. Navigate to the Bank section.

2. Select the specific bank for which you want to retrieve previous transactions.

3. Click on the Bank Import >> Bank Feeds option.

4. Select View History from the dropdown menu.

5. Under Bank Feeds, enter the From Date and To Date for the period of transactions you want to retrieve.

6. Click on Get Transactions to retrieve the transactions for the specified period.
