### How can we show CIS deducted amount while raising an Invoice?

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 12 Nov, 2021 at  2:54 PM

		You may deduct the CIS amount while creating the invoice itself by selecting "CIS Control Account" from the action drop down available under payment section.

Navigation: Bookkeeping >> Select Client >> Sales > > Invoices >> + New Invoice

once clicking the + New invoice button

Enter the information as needed.
