### How to delete the bank explained transactions in Bulk

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 22 Oct, 2025 at  2:05 PM

		If you have imported transactions from your bank that you no longer need or want to keep in your records, you can easily delete them in bulk. Here's how you can do it:

1. Navigate to the Bank section on your dashboard.

2. Select the specific bank from which you want to delete the transactions.

3. Choose the custom period for which you want to delete the transactions.

4. Select the type (for ex: Journal).

5. Check the box for all the transactions you want to delete.

6. Click on the delete button to remove the selected transactions.

Once you have deleted the transactions, you may also want to delete the bank imports to ensure that they are completely removed from your records. Here's how you can do that:

1. Go to the Bank Import section.

2. Click on History to view all the imported transactions.

3. Select the specific import you want to delete.

4. Choose the delete option from the actions menu.

By following these steps, you can easily delete bank imports and transactions that you no longer need in your records. If you have any further questions or need assistance, feel free to reach out to our customer support team for help.
