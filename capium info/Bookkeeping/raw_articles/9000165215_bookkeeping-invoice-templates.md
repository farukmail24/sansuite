### Bookkeeping Invoice Templates

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 18 May, 2020 at 11:57 AM

		Location:  Bookkeeping module > Dashboard > Client specific > Settings > General Settings > Invoice Templates

Refer to the link

You can select a theme for the invoice template, and once selected, will apply to all invoices.

Click the preview button to preview the invoice template. Edit button to Edit the template. Whilst editing the invoice template you can add Bank Account details, VAT No. etc.

Word format Invoice template can also be downloaded from the action drop-down. It’s a zip folder consisting of the templates Invoice, Credit Note, Dividend and Quotation. All of which can also be edited and uploaded.
