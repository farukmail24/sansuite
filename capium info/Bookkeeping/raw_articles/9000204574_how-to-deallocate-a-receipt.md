### How to deallocate a receipt

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 20 Jul, 2021 at  4:42 PM

		Location:

Contacts >> Customers >> Go into customers account >> Find the invoice that the receipt has been allocated to >> Use the drop down box to select 'Unallocated amount'.

Video Tutorial:

Click here to watch how to deallocate a receipt 

Help Guide:

From the navigation panel, go into 'Contacts' and then 'Customers'. 

Using the search function, find the customer and go into their account. 

Find the invoice that the receipt has been allocated to and using the action drop down box, click on 'Unallocated Amount' 

Method 2:

You can choose to delete the receipt if need be. 

Go into 'Sales' and then 'Receipts' from the navigation panel 

Find the receipt and and using the action drop down box, click on 'delete'.
