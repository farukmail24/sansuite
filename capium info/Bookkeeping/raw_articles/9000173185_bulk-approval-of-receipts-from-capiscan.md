### Bulk Approval of Receipts from DocScan

			Print

Click here to watch how to perform bulk approval of receipts

Help/Guide:

Navigate to the general Bookkeeping Dashboard and select DocScan from the menu on the left panel.

Once in DocScan, you will find a list of all scanned receipts from your clients there. 

You will also find filters at the top to narrow down your search.

 Use the action drop down menu on the right hand side to Record or Reject those receipts.

Record will add the receipt to your clients' purchases.
