### How do I view the journal entries that are saved as draft?

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 18 Nov, 2021 at  2:33 PM

		Navigation: Bookkeeping > Select Client > Tasks > Journals

Draft journals will appear in "Red". 

Please select edit from the action dropdown, and then balance the journal

You may view the journal entries under Task>>Journals section and you need to balance the journal entry amounts to reflect on the trial balance.
