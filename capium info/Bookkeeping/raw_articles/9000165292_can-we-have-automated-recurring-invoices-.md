### Can we have automated recurring invoices?

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 22 Oct, 2021 at  3:35 PM

		Yes, 

Please go to Bookkeeping > Sales > Recurring Invoices

Click on + Recurring Invoice 

The next page which will appear looks similar to the normal sales input

To set the repeating invoice fill in the Repeat After choose the amount and either weeks or months

Please proceed to fill out the other fields, as necessary. Then click save and close

This invoice will now be automatically created.

If you would like to change the date of the recurring invoice, you may delete the recurring invoice and add the new recurring invoice with the desired date. 

Click here to watch how to stop recurring purchases
