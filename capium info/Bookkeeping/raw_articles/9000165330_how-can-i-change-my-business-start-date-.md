### How can I change my business start date?

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 17 May, 2021 at  2:05 PM

Navigation: My Admin > My Firm > Clients

Help/Guide 

Please head to you client list in My Admin

Click on the blue pencil button seen below

Once in the client area, click on the purple pencil next to edit client

A pop up will appear. Please proceed to change the business start date

Please click save at the bottom of the pop-up
