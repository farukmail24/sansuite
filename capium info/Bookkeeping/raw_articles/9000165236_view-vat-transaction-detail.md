Help/Guide 

On VAT transaction detail page, you can choose the period as a Current month, Last month, Current year, Last year. On the basis of the period chosen date will automatically change.

You can also filter by contact and ref No. and search invoice.

You can export the VAT report in PDF and Excel format

This page contains detailed VAT report transaction wise.
