### How can we record Daily Shop Sales (Cards and Cash Sales)

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 18 Nov, 2021 at  2:34 PM

		Navigation: Bookkeeping > Select Client > Tasks > Journals

Watch how to record daily shop sales here

There are two quick ways to do this:

1. You may add the Cash Sales as a Journal from Bookkeeping>>Tasks>>Journals. In a Journal you would Debit the Account for "Cash in Hand" and Credit the "Sales" Account.

2. Alternatively, you may record under Sales for Bank receipts. Navigate through. Bookkeeping>>Sales>>Receipts then click on "+Receipt".
