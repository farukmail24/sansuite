### Chart of Accounts (COA)

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 18 Jan, 2021 at  4:34 PM

		There are two variations of Capium's Chart of Accounts; Client Specific & Master

Client Specific COA - changes made will only apply to a specific client... this includes new, edited or deleted COA codes

Location: Bookkeeping module > Dashboard > Client dashboard > Settings> Chart of Accounts

Refer to the link

Master COA - changes made here will apply to all clients... this includes new, edited or deleted COA codes

Location: Bookkeeping module > General Settings > Chart of Accounts 

Refer to the link

To view the client specific chart of accounts, follow the navigation above, and on clicking '+ Account', you can add a new Chart of Account code.

 You can also edit the existing codes in the default COA by clicking on the Action drop-down menu and clicking Edit.

To view the Master specific chart of accounts, follow the navigation above, and on clicking '+ Account', you can add a new Chart of Account code.
