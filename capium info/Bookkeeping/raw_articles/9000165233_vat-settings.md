### VAT Settings

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 3 Jul, 2020 at  2:50 PM

		Location:  Bookkeeping module > Dashboard > Client specific > VAT > VAT settings

Click here to watch how to add a VAT code

Click here to watch how to configure VAT Settings

The VAT settings page you may simply check the details related to:

VAT Scheme: The different VAT schemes are listed under the drop-down such as Accrual Based, Flat Rate Accrual based, Flat rate cash based etc. you may update the same here or under Company Info.

Registration date: It lists the date on which the firm was VAT registered

Registration No: The registration number of the firm. Please note that the registration number should be correct otherwise you may face an Error while submission i.e. 1046 ‘ Authentication Failure’.

VAT Submit Type: The type of submission i.e. Monthly, Quarterly. Half yearly or Annually can be selected from the drop-down.
