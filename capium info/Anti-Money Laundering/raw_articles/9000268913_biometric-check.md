### Biometric Check

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 9 Jul, 2025 at  4:56 PM

		Companies House is implementing mandatory identity verification checks for individuals registering or managing UK companies, including directors, people with significant control (PSCs), and those filing information on behalf of a company.

The new Biometric verification feature is now available through Capium, in partnership with Veriphy. This integration allows accountants to seamlessly complete biometric checks within the Capium platform, enhancing security and streamlining compliance workflows. 

The check is priced at £2.50 plus vat per check.

Our biometric checks have been created to make verifying client identity much more efficient and effective. They allow you to:

Align your customer due diligence processes with internal risk assessments
Avoid penalties that can cause financial and reputational harm to your firm
Protect your firm from fraudsters
Reduce time-to-revenue by streamlining the client onboarding process
 - Gain ultimate peace of mind of remote and face-to-face client identity through state-of-the-art government-standard biometric technology

Biometric Check

Navigation: My Admin > My Firm > AML

To perform a Biometric check click on 'Perform Check' then 'Biometric Check' as shown on the picture below. 

You will now be directed to the Biometric Check page, where you can input either your own details or your client's to begin the verification process.

You will now need to enter the mandatory information into the system:

Gender 
Title
First Name
Surname 
Date of Birth

Email Address
UK Mobile Number  

If you don't have a reference number you can click on 'I don't wish to enter a reference'.

Once all mandatory personal details have been entered into the system, click ‘Perform Check’. This will generate and send an Email/SMS to the appropriate recipient, either yourself or your client, based on whose information was provided for the biometric verification.  

*Disclaimer: Capium does not store sensitive personal data, will only pass it to third party for verification and provide you access to the report as an outcome.

You or your client will now receive an Email/SMS from Veriphy to complete an identity verification check on behalf of Veriphy.  

This step requires you to:

Be in a well-lit room
Scan or upload an identity document, avoiding glare and reflections 
Take a selfie 

To do this click on 'here' in the Email or the link in your SMS.

*Disclaimer: There's no need to log into Veriphy to complete the biometric check. 

Once the Biometric Check has been completed, you can easily review the results within the My Admin portal. 

Navigation: My Admin > My Firm > AML

Here, you'll find a list of all compliance checks previously conducted, with the most recent appearing at the top. 

To view the result of the Biometric Check, click on the '3 dots' under the Action column, either select 'View PDF or Download PDF'. 

Opening or downloading the report will automatically update the Result tab on the page, ensuring your records remain current. 

*Disclaimer: There's no need to log into Veriphy to view your result.
