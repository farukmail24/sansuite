### Biometric AML Checks

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 19 Jun, 2025 at  5:02 PM

		Capium now integrates with Veriphy to offer secure, biometric identity verification for individuals managing UK companies — including directors, people with significant control (PSCs), and company filers.

This service helps your firm meet new Companies House regulations, which require verification of a person’s identity using photographic ID (such as a passport or driving licence), matched against a live biometric (facial recognition) check.

With Capium + Veriphy, these checks are conducted seamlessly, securely, and in full compliance with AML legislation and Companies House expectations.

 Compliant – Meets new UK identity verification standards Secure – Uses bank-grade facial recognition technology Seamless – Integrated directly into your Capium dashboard

You can now perform AML biometric checks directly from Capium, without switching systems — giving you greater confidence in your compliance processes and helping your clients meet their obligations.
