### Enabling &#39;Rounding up to Nearest Amount&#39; in the Charity Module

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 14 Nov, 2025 at  3:29 PM

		Title: Enabling 'Rounding up to Nearest Amount' in the Charity Module

1. Access the Charity Section: Begin by logging into your account and proceeding to the Charity section.

2. Within the Charity section, select Client and then click on Manage to access the Charity Preferences.

3. Charity Preferences: In the Charity Preferences area, navigate to Accounts Production to locate the 'Rounding up to Nearest Amount' option.

4. Enable the Option: In the Accounts Production section, you will see the 'Rounding up to Nearest Amount' option. Please check the box adjacent to this option to activate it.

5. Save Changes: After enabling the option, ensure that you save your changes before exiting the Charity Preferences section.

6. Regenerate Accounts Report: Following the activation of 'Rounding up to Nearest Amount', it is essential to regenerate or create the accounts report to confirm that the changes have been successfully implemented.

If you have adhered to these steps and continue to encounter difficulties, please do not hesitate to contact our customer support team for further assistance. We are committed to providing support and ensuring that you can effectively adjust your Charity Preferences.
