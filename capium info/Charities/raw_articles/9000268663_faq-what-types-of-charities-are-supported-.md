### FAQ: What types of charities are supported?

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 3 Jun, 2025 at 11:02 AM

		Capium’s Charity Module supports the following types of UK-based charities:

Charitable Companies – Registered with both Companies House and the Charity Commission, typically limited by guarantee.

Charitable Incorporated Organisations (CIOs) – Registered only with the Charity Commission, offering a corporate structure without Companies House filing obligations.

Unincorporated Charities – Such as trusts and associations, which are registered with the Charity Commission but not Companies House.
