### How to add accountants details in Charity accounts report

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 21 Nov, 2025 at  1:26 PM

		For charities, an Accountant’s Report is not applicable.

Instead, the correct document to use is the Independent Examiner’s Report, as required under charity reporting regulations.

Please use the Independent Examiner’s Report for your filing.

Please navigate to Charity module >> Client Specific >>  Accounts Production >> Independent Examiner’s Report. Once done, you can re-generate the accounts report or generate the new report.

If you have any further questions or need assistance, please do not hesitate to reach out to our customer support team. We’re here to help!
