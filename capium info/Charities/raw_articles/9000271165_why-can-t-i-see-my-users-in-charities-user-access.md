### Why can&#39;t I see my users in Charities? - User Access

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 17 Sep, 2025 at 10:44 AM

Charity - Users

Navigation: Capium Home > 'C' icon > Charity > Manage 

Users will show up in Manage > Users after they have entered the Charity module. To do so, they simply need to click the ‘C’ icon at the top and access the Charity module at least once. Following that, they will be automatically included in the Users list.
