### How to regenerate or refresh the accounts report in Charity module

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 22 Oct, 2025 at  8:43 AM

To regenerate the accounts report, please follow these steps:

1. Navigate to Charity.

2. Go to Client Specific.

3. Select Accounts Production.

4. Click on Annual Accounts.

5. Choose the relevant Period.

6. Click View.

7. Select Re-generate.

Should you experience any additional challenges or have further inquiries, please feel free to contact our customer support team for assistance.
