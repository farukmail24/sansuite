### AP: Adding Company Secretaries

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 25 Jun, 2026 at  3:06 PM

### Overview

The Secretaries section allows you to add and manage company secretaries within Accounts Production. Secretary information can be maintained for company records and can also be displayed on the Company's Information page within generated reports.

### Navigation

Accounts Production > Settings > Report Settings > Company Contacts > Secretaries

### Step 1: Add a New Secretary

Click + Add New Secretary.

A Contact Details page will open where you can enter the secretary's information.

Complete the relevant secretary information, including the secretary's name and any additional details required.

Once all information has been entered, click Save.

### Step 2: Display the Secretary on Company Reports

If you would like the secretary's name to appear on the Company's Information page within generated reports, select the Show on Company Information Page checkbox.

### 

### Step 3: Edit or Delete a Secretary

You can manage existing secretary records using the available actions.

 - Edit – Update the secretary's information.

 - Delete – Remove the secretary from the company records.
