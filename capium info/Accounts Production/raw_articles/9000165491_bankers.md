### Bankers

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 14 Oct, 2020 at  5:00 PM

		Location: Accounts Production module > Settings > Report Settings > Company Contact > Bankers

A person who conducts the business of banking, individually or as a member of a company, keeps an establishment for the deposit or loan of money, or for traffic in money, bills of exchange, etc.

You may add the name of Bankers of the company by clicking on ‘+ Add New Banker’. As soon as the tab is clicked contact detail page of secretary appears. Name of the Banker and the other relevant details are to be filled and saved.

If you want the name of the Banker to be displayed on the Reports of the company, then the checkbox of ‘Show on Company Information page’ is ticked.

Names of all Bankers with their respective details will be listed below.

Actions that can be taken either edit or delete as per the requirement.
