### How do I include / exclude an accountant&#39;s report?

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 31 Dec, 2021 at  2:26 PM

		Navigation: Accounts Production > Client > Reports > Report Settings.

Click here to watch how to add/remove reports

You can customise what is in your annual accounts by clicking on the button report settings once you have created your annual accounts

The below pop up will show.

To remove the accountants report please uncheck the box next to accountants report.

Then click save.

Please then click on the report you would like to edit.

Click on the regenerate button to show the changes.
