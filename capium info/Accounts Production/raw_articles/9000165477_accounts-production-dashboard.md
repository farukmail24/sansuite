### Accounts Production Dashboard

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 18 Aug, 2020 at  4:47 PM

		Location: Accounts Production module > Client Specific

This is the main landing page of the Accounts Production module displaying the general overview of various reports prepared and their status of submission to Companies House.

On the top left of the page the name of the company in respect of which reports are to be prepared. Then comes the registration details, address, trading status (trading or dormant company) and audit status. These details can be edited by clicking on the ‘Edit’ tab. When you click on ‘Edit’ tab user will be redirected to the ‘Settings >> Dashboard’ section of the account production.

List of reports that are prepared can be seen in the next two sections. 

In the second section, the list of Annual reports is prepared for the company with its status of submission to Companies House. The third section is related to the Management Report prepared for the company. As you click on the reference number of the report it will show the overview of the report. By clicking on ‘All Reports’ it will redirect the user to the ‘Reports’ section of the software.

If no reports are prepared, you will see a message which states “No saved reports found. Click here to add a new report. By clicking on ‘Click Here’ you can add a new report which will redirect you to the ‘Reports’ section of the software.
