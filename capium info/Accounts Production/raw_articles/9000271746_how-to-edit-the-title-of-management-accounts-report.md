### How to edit the title of Management Accounts Report

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 23 Oct, 2025 at  9:10 AM

		Title: Modifying Management Reports

At times, you may need to modify the wording of your management reports. Here's how you can do it:

1. Navigate to Accounts production

2. Select the specific client for whom you want to modify the report

3. Go to Settings

4. Click on Report Settings

5. Under Company info, select Edit

6. Update the box labeled management report name with the desired wording

7. Once you have completed these steps, regenerate the management accounts

If you encounter any issues or need further assistance with modifying the management reports, please don't hesitate to reach out to our customer support team. We are here to help and ensure that you have a smooth experience with our software.
