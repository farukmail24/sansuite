### Accounting Policy

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 12 Oct, 2020 at  5:43 PM

		Location: Accounts Production module > Settings > Report Setting > Accounting Policy

This Policy is part of Notes to Accounts. The user can select different headings which will form part of notes to accounts. When user tick on the checkbox that part will become editable and the user can edit its contents if required.

This part includes different notes related to both Profit and Loss Account and for Balance Sheet.

You may select the respective fields which are to form part of Notes to Accounts or edit if required.
