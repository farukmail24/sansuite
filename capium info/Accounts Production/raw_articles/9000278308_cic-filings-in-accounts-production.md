### CIC Filings in Accounts Production

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 17 Aug, 2026 at  1:30 PM

		A community interest company (CIC) files a CIC 34 report alongside its annual accounts. In Capium, the annual accounts are produced exactly as they are for any other limited company client, and the CIC 34 report is prepared separately and packaged with them before filing.

This article is the overview of the process. Each step has its own article with the full detail.

### 
The CIC features in Capium

Once a client is flagged as a CIC, three features appear in Accounts Production for that client.

FeatureWhere it appearsWhat it doesCIC 34 NotesSettingsHolds the content of each part of the CIC 34 reportCIC ReportsReportsGenerates the CIC 34 report from the notesCIC Submissions WizardTasksBuilds the zip package and links through to Companies House
None of these appear until the CIC option is ticked against the client.

### The process in order

### 
Points to be aware of

The accounts side of the process is unchanged. Accounting periods, trial balance, report settings and annual accounts all work as they do for any limited company client.
The CIC 34 report covers the current financial year only. There is no comparative column and no prior year content to complete.
Nothing in the CIC 34 Notes is populated automatically. Every part is entered manually for each client, each year.
There is no direct or API submission for CIC filings. Capium prepares and packages the reports, and the upload and payment take place on the Companies House website.
