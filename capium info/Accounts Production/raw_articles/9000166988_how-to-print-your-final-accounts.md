### How to print your final Accounts

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 18 Apr, 2019 at 12:01 PM

		To print a copy of your Accounts, head to Reports >> Annual Accounts 

Then navigate to the Accounts, click the Action drop down and download as PDF.

Then open the PDF document and print.
