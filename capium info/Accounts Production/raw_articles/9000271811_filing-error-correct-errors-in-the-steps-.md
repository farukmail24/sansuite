### Filing Error "Correct errors in the steps"

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 22 Oct, 2025 at  9:39 AM

		Title: Filing Error: "Correct errors in the steps"

If you are experiencing a filing error while using Capium, there are a few steps you can take to troubleshoot the issue:

1. Clear your browsing history: Sometimes, a build-up of cookies and cached data can cause issues with submitting files. Clearing your browsing history can help to resolve this issue.

2. Use Google Chrome: We recommend using Google Chrome as your browser when submitting files through Capium. Other browsers may not be fully compatible with the platform, leading to potential filing errors.

3. Submit from a Private window: Ensure that you are submitting your files from a incognito window only for this submission, rather than a normal window.

If you have followed these steps and are still experiencing filing errors, please reach out to our customer support team for further assistance. We are here to help troubleshoot any issues you may be experiencing and ensure that your submissions are processed successfully.
