### How do you add a draft watermark to your accounts

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 5 Aug, 2021 at  3:15 PM

		Navigation: Accounts Production > Select Client > Reports > Annual Accounts

Please click here to watch how to add a watermark to your accounts

Help/Guide: 

When creating a set of accounts you can download them with a draft watermark.

Please first create your annual accounts.

Please click here to find out how to create a set of annual accounts

Once your accounts are created please click on the green report settings button

The following pop up will appear

please click 'show draft message' then click save

After saving please click on the report you would like the draft mark on and click the regenerate button.

Once the report is regenerated please download the PDF by clicking on the blue PDF button

The following watermark will now be shown on the PDF report
