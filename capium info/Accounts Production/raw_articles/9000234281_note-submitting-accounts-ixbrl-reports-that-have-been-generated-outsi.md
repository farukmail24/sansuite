### Note: Submitting Accounts/ixbrl Reports that have been generated outside of Capium

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 19 Dec, 2023 at 10:16 AM

		Capium can not guarantee that any accounts or ixbrl reports that have been generated outside out of Capium, can be successfully submitted to HMRC/Companies House.

This is because if the accounts/reports get rejected,  we not have any tools to found out why they have been rejected, as they have been created outside of our eco-system. Therefore we can investigate the issue further.
