### Directors cessation date is not showing in company information or directors report. How can we correct this?

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 26 Jun, 2025 at  4:07 PM

		Welcome to this quick guide on how to show the directors cessation date on the directors report. This guide will walk you through the essential steps.

Accounts Production: Company Contacts 

Navigation: Accounts production > Client Specific > Settings > Report Settings > Company Directors 

First you will need to add in the director cessation date. To do this click on the blue icon shown in the picture below. 

This will now open up a new tab shown below, you can now enter the date ceased for this specific director. 

Click 'Save' to save the changes.  

Accounts Production: Annual Accounts

Navigation: Accounts production > Client Specific >Reports > Annual Accounts/Management Accounts  > Directors Report

After you added the directors cessation date you can create your annual accounts/management accounts, in this you will see the directors cessation date next to that specific director. 

Accounts Production: Company Info

Navigation: Accounts production > Client Specific > Settings > Report Settings > Company Info

If you want to show resigned directors, in your annual accounts/management accounts, click on 'Show resigned directors'.
