### Solicitors

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 14 Oct, 2020 at  5:01 PM

		Location: Accounts Production module > Settings > Report Settings > Company Contact > Solicitors

Solicitor deals with conveyancing, drawing up of wills, and other legal matters. He may instruct barristers and represent clients in some courts.

You may add the name of Solicitors of the company by clicking on ‘+ Add New Solicitor’ button. As soon as the tab is clicked contact detail page of solicitor appears. Name of the Solicitor and the other relevant details are to be filled and saved.

If you want the name of the Solicitor to be displayed on the Reports of the company, then the checkbox of ‘Show on Company Information page’ is to be ticked.

Names of all Solicitors with their respective details will be listed below.

Actions that can be taken either edit or delete as per the requirement.
