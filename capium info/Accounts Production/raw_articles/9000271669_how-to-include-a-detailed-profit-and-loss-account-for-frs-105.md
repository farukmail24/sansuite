### How to Include a Detailed Profit and Loss Account for FRS 105

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 21 Oct, 2025 at  2:52 PM

		FRS 105: How to Include a Detailed Profit and Loss Account

If you are using FRS 105 'Full accounts' and would like to include a 'Detailed Profit and Loss Account' in your annual accounts report, you can easily do so by following these steps:

1. Navigate to the Accounts Production module.

2. Select Client Specific from the menu.

3. Click on Reports, then Annual Accounts.

4. In the Report Settings, look for the option labelled 'Detailed Income Statement'.

5. Check the box next to 'Detailed Income Statement' to include the detailed profit and loss account in your report.

Once you have completed these steps, don't forget to regenerate the accounts report to ensure that the changes take effect.
