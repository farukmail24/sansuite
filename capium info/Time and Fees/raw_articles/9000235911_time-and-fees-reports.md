### Time and Fees: Reports

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 1 Feb, 2024 at 11:01 AM

Navigation: Time and Fees > Reports

Click to redirect to the above navigation

The Time and Fees Reports section offers a comprehensive breakdown of your practice's profitability and productivity across different categories, including time, expenses, invoices, and user-specific metrics.

Time:

The Time Report includes:

Client

Job ID

Task

User

Hours

Amount (GBP)

Cost (GBP)

Profit (GBP)

The Report can be customised based on the filters provided, which include:

Hours (All, Billable and Non Billable)

Group by (Client, Job, Task, User, Date)

Filters (Clients, Tasks, Users, Jobs)

Period (This Week, Last Week, This Month, Last Month, This Quarter, Last Quarter, This Year, Last Year and Custom)

Expenses:

The Expenses Report includes:

Client

ID

Task

Type

User

Amount (GBP)

The Report can be customised based on the filters provided, which include:

Expenses (All, Billable and Non Billable)

Group by (Client, Task, Expense Type, User, Date)

Filters (Clients, Tasks, Users, Expense Type)

Period (This Week, Last Week, This Month, Last Month, This Quarter, Last Quarter, This Year, Last Year and Custom)

Invoices:

The Invoices Report includes:

ID

Client

Date

Status

Last Paid On

Invoiced (GBP)

Paid (GBP)

Balance (GBP)

The Report can be customised based on the filters provided, which include:

Filters (Clients, Status)

Period (This Week, Last Week, This Month, Last Month, This Quarter, Last Quarter, This Year, Last Year and Custom)

Users:

The Users Report includes:

User

Total Hours

Amount (GBP)

Cost (GBP)

Profit (GBP)

The Report can be customised based on the filters provided, which include:

User (Contractors, Employees)

Period (This Week, Last Week, This Month, Last Month, This Quarter, Last Quarter, This Year, Last Year and Custom)

All Reports can be exported to Excel, CSV or PDF.

If you encounter any issues or require further assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to your Onboarding Manager.
