### Why can&#39;t I see my users in Time and Fees?

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 15 Sep, 2025 at  5:00 PM

Time and Fees - Users 

Navigation: Capium Home > 'C' icon > Time and Fees > Manage 

Users will appear in Manage > Users once they’ve accessed the Time and Fees module. To do this, they just need to click the ‘C’ icon at the top and open the Time and Fees module at least once. After that, they will automatically be added to the Users list.
