### Time and Fees: Accountant and Staff Access 

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 12 Mar, 2024 at  4:14 PM

		This article will provide a step by step guide on how to give accountants and staff access to the Time and Fees Module

Please note that the main admin is able to do the following.

Location: Time and Fees > Manage > Permissions 

Here you can select access for:

Admin

Manager

Staff

Within the categories you are able to set specific permissions for:

Time

Jobs

Fees

Expenses 

Reports

Manage

Setting

Activity 

Announcement
