### Practice Management Client Details Screen

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 8 Aug, 2019 at  3:00 PM

		Location: Practice Management module > Workspace > Clients 

You can view/edit/add/remove Client Details. 
 
Help/Guide:

 - You can view/edit/add/copy the client's contact information, company information, Services offering, Business information, PAYE details, and Company Directors.  
 - You may update the data in the relevant fields which are provided by default. 
 - Also, there is an option to add the custom fields to update the additional info of your choice. 
 - With the help of integration between the modules, data will be reflected here upon updating anywhere in the other module dashboards. 
 - There is a validation check to comply that the correct characters are entered the fields, to ensure data entered is in an acceptable format. Eg: PAYE Ref No. 947/A949895 (Alpha-Numeric & character limit).
