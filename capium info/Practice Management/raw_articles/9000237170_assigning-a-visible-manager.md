### Assigning a visible manager

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 26 Mar, 2024 at  2:41 PM

		Location: Practice Management > Practice > Team > Select Team Member to check Visible Manager.

Managers who can approve the staff timesheet can be assigned as shown.

 Staff members who record their time as either:

 - Client Billable
 - Non-Client Billable OR
 - Other Non-Billable can be approved by the Visible Manager.
