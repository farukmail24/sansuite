Help/Guide:

Services can be assigned to clients in bulk using the Assign Services feature and is a per-requisite to creating deadlines in Practice Management

To assign Services to clients;

Navigate to Settings > Services and click on Assign Services as shown below

### 

### Assigning 

In this page, you will find a grid view of all clients on the left and all clients above, simply tick the checkbox(s) to assign various services to your clients in bulk.
