### How to set up custom templates for Letters of Engagement/Proposals

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 5 Oct, 2020 at  4:33 PM

		Location: Practice Management module > Settings > Document Templates

Refer to the link

Help/Guide: 

Once in Document Templates, click 'New Template'

Enter the Template name, choose between an Engagement or Proposal Letter and click Continue 

Paste your custom Letter of Engagement/Proposal template here and ensure to not delete any of the tags which are as follows; [proposal.price_summary], [practice.name], [client.name], [proposal.commencement_date] & [proposal.end_date].  Insert those tags in areas that are grammatically correct as the system will use the tags to pick up information about the client and/or your practice.

Paste your custom email here, again ensure none of the tags are deleted. Once happy, click save.
