### How does Capium bookkeeping link to MTD IT submissions?

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 27 Jan, 2026 at  7:30 PM

		Transactions recorded in Capium bookkeeping are summarised and mapped into the MTD IT categories used for quarterly submissions. The data is cumulative from the start of the tax year.
