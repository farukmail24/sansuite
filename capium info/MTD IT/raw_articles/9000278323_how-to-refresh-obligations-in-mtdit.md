### How to Refresh Obligations in MTDIT

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 18 Aug, 2026 at  4:25 PM

		Welcome to this quick guide on how to refresh obligations in MTDIT. This guide will walk you through the essential steps.  

Refresh Obligations  

Navigation: MTDIT > Manage > Client Specific > Sources

To refresh your obligations, first navigate to the MTDIT module.

 - Select Manage and choose the client you want to refresh the obligations for.
 - Navigate to Sources.
 - Click Refresh Obligations.
