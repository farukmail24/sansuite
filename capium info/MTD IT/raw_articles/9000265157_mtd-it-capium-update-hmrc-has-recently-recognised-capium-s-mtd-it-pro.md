### MTD IT  - Capium Update - HMRC has recently recognised Capium’s MTD IT product.

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 15 Oct, 2025 at  3:34 PM

		HMRC has recently recognised Capium’s MTD IT product. You can find us listed here: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/find-software-thats-compatible-with-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax.

We’ve invited a select group of customers to test the product and join HMRC’s beta pilot scheme. Their journey will be documented and shared with our entire community as we prepare for the full release of our bespoke MTD IT solution later in 2025 – this will be well-publicised, so keep an eye out for updates.

If you’re interested in how we can help your clients keep digital records, scan receipts, and manage invoices seamlessly, we’d be happy to book you onto a 365 demo. https://www.capium.com/book-a-demo/

Please don’t hesitate to raise a ticket out if you have any further questions. https://www.capium.com/products/capium365/
