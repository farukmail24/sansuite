### Capium’s New MTD IT Module and Capium 365: Preparing for
MTD IT

The introduction of Making Tax Digital for Income Tax (MTD IT) is one of the most significant changes to the UK tax system in recent years. At Capium, we’re committed to helping accountants and their clients stay ahead of these changes with the right tools, guidance, and support.

More information about MTD IT can be found in this webinar we held recently: https://www.bigmarker.com/capium/making-mtd-it-work-for-you-who-s-affected-how-to-register

### Our New MTD IT Module

We’re excited to announce that we are building a new MTD IT module to handle both quarterly submissions and the final submission required under MTD IT.

Clients enrolled in HMRC’s pilot scheme are already making submissions via our new product. Over the coming months, we’ll be running a series of emails and webinars to keep you updated as we move closer to launch.

The new module is planned to be commercially available in early 2026 and will be charged at the same rate as our existing compliance products.

Find out more about how to join the Capium Pilot Scheme, which is open now:Capium Pilot Scheme Guide
