### MTD IT Product Showcase Webinar - 19/02

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 27 Feb, 2026 at 12:44 PM

		Webinar Link

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax (MTD IT) is approaching fast, and for many practices the key challenge is choosing the right MTD solution for each client, not just meeting the deadline.This practical Capium webinar focuses entirely on MTD IT. We’ll walk through the different MTD solutions Capium offers and explain how to match each option to different client types; from spreadsheet-based clients to digitally confident, multi-income individuals.Rather than a broad product overview, this session is designed to give you clarity and confidence in deciding who should use what, and why.What we’ll cover:The different MTD IT solutions available within CapiumHow to assess clients by digital readiness, complexity, and behaviourWhich Capium MTD solution best suits:Spreadsheet-inclined clientsClients ready for basic cloud bookkeepingTech-forward or multi-income clientsBest-practice approaches for managing mixed client portfolios under MTD ITHow to prepare now to avoid disruption laterThis webinar is ideal for firms that want a structured, practical approach to MTD IT, with the flexibility to support different client needs without overcomplicating their workflows.
