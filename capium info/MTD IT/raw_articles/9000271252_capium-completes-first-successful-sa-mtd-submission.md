### Capium Completes First Successful SA MTD Submission

We can confirm that  Capium has successfully enabled practices to complete their first Self-Assessment (SA) Making Tax Digital (MTD) quarterly returns for both UK Property income and Self-Employment tax. These submissions were made under HMRC’s live pilot programme.

This represents a major milestone not just for Capium, but for the broader move to Making Tax Digital for Income Tax (MTD IT) — a reform that will require quarterly returns rather than an annual filing. Capium’s success shows that both its software and its clients are ready ahead of the April 2026 mandate.
