### MTD IT - How to allow software to connect with HMRC

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 13 May, 2026 at  7:46 AM

		To submit under MTD IT, you must first authorise the software to connect with HM Revenue & Customs. This process securely links your Agent Services Account (ASA) with the software, allowing it to interact with HMRC on your client’s behalf. 

Navigation: MTD IT >Manage > Select  Client >Start Authorisation

1. Open the MTD IT 

From your dashboard, search for and open the relevant client.

Click Start Authorisation 

Click on Authorise with HMRC (this may appear as Authorise Capium MTD IT).

You can also bulk authorise your clients as well. Please click here to view the article on how to bulk authorisation. 

2. HMRC Authorisation Process

    Once clicked on Start Authorisation

    You will be redirected to the HRMC authorisation page, select Continue

    Click Sign in to the HRMC Online services.

### 3. Complete HMRC Authorisation

    Sign in using your Agent Services Account (ASA) credentials

    Once signed in this will allow the software to connect with HRMC

    Click Give permission.

For the next step, once you have signed up you have to prove your identity with HMRC providing the following information. 

4. HMRC Confirmation & Status Update

    Once the authorisation is approved, HMRC will confirm the request.

    You will then be redirected back to Capium automatically.

    The client’s MTD IT status should update to Authorised.

Note: If the status does not update immediately, refresh the page or re-open the client record to confirm the authorisation has been successfully applied.

You will  be able to review and verify the client’s income sources and proceed with setting up the tax return within the system.
