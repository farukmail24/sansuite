### Do I need to submit if there is no income or expenses?

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 6 Feb, 2026 at 10:12 AM

		Yes, you are required to submit a nil quarterly submission even if your business has no income or expenses during that period. This is an important part of complying with HMRC (Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs) regulations. Below, we’ll explore why this is necessary and how to go about it.

Why Submit a Nil Return?

1. Compliance with HMRC Regulations: Submitting a nil return ensures that you are fulfilling your legal obligations as a business owner. HMRC requires all registered businesses to report their financial activities, even if there are none to report.

2. Avoiding Penalties: Failing to submit a nil return can lead to penalties and fines from HMRC. By submitting on time, you help protect from unnecessary financial burdens.

3. Maintaining Your Tax Records: Regular submissions, even if they are nil, help maintain accurate tax records. This can be beneficial for future reference and ensures that your business remains in good standing with HMRC.

In summary, submitting a nil quarterly return is a crucial part of your responsibilities as a business owner. It ensures compliance with HMRC, helps avoid penalties, and maintains accurate tax records.
