### What is the difference between Book Start & Business Start date?

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 23 Aug, 2021 at 12:40 PM

		A Book Start Date is simply the date to which your client(s) began trading.

The Business Start Date refers to the date the clients' company was incorporated.
