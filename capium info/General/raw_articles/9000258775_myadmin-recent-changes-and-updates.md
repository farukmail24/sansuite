### MyAdmin: Recent Changes and Updates

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 30 Dec, 2024 at  2:12 PM

		Video: Recent changes in Myadmin

Navigation: My Admin > Firm Details

When you start working on Capium for the first time, it is essential to complete the mandatory information in the Firm Details section. Below is a guide to the fields you’ll need to fill out, including recent changes and updates.

Summary of Recent Changes:

Secondary Email - You can now add an additional email address for each client. 

Addresses - You can now add multiple address types; Registered Office, Trading Address and Overseas Address.

SIC Codes - By entering the Companies House Reference Number, the system will automatically fetch the Company codes. These codes can also link with the Company Secretarial module and all addresses (Registered Office, Trading, and Overseas) are synced automatically. 

If you encounter any issues or require further assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to your account manager.
