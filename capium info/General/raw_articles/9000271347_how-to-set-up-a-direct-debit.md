### How to set up a Direct Debit

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 26 Sep, 2025 at  1:34 PM

		Welcome to this quick guide on how to set up a Direct Debit. This guide will walk you through the essential steps.

Direct Debit

Navigation:  My Admin > My Subscription > Subscription > Payment Methods

To set up a Direct Debit navigate to My Admin, click on My Subscription then click on subscription.  

This will take you to the Subscription Portal, navigate to Payment Methods and set up your Direct Debit.
