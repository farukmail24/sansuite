### Submission Credentials for Accounts and Corporation Tax filing

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 11 Jul, 2024 at  7:50 AM

		See below the credentials required for accounts and corporation tax filing:

Your companies House Presenter ID - The Presenter Identifier starts with 000 and ends with 000, is a mixture of numbers and letters and is 11 characters long. This is usually entered in capitals.

You Company authentication code - 6 digit

You Company's Government gateway credentials -  Can be up to 12 characters

More information can be found here: https://capium.freshdesk.com/a/solutions/articles/9000236664
