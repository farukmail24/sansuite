### How to pay for Capium using GoCardless or Stripe

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 9 Jul, 2021 at  2:55 PM

		Location

My Admin >> My Subscription >> Invoice Summary >> on the invoice that needs to be paid select either 'GC' for GoCardless or 'SP' for Stripe.

Video Tutorials:

Click here to watch how to set up a payment using GoCardless
Click here to watch how to set up a payment using Stripe 

Help Guide:

You can pay for your Capium invoices through a credit or debit card using Stripe payment, alternatively you can set up a Direct Debit using GoCardless.

If setting up a GoCardless all occurring invoices will be taken automatically by Direct Debit. You will get an email reminder before the payment will be taken.

Navigating to your invoices

To find the invoices that need to be paid, use the admin button on the top right hand side of your screen to go into your admin panel.

Using the navigation tabs on the top of your screen, go to 'My Subscription'.

Go into either 'Invoice Summary' or 'Other Invoices' to see all of your outstanding invoices.

Payments using GoCardless

Click on the 'GC' button to bring up the payment page.

Put in your bank information and click on 'Set up Direct Debit' to make the payment.

Payments using Stripe 

Click on the 'SP' button to bring up the payment page.

Enter your bank details and click on 'Confirm & Pay' to make the payment.
