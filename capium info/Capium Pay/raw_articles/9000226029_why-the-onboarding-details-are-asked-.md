### Why the onboarding details are asked?

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 6 Apr, 2023 at  8:15 AM

		Capium pay is powered by UNIPaaS who is a regulated payment institute by the FCA. As part of the KYC/KYB (Know Your Customer/Business) process, we are verifying the identity of all vendors and their bank accounts so eventually, we will be able to pay the vendors the funds collected on behalf of the service provided.
