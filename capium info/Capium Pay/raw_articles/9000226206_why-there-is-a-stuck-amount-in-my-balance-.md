### Why there is a stuck amount in my balance?

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 5 Apr, 2023 at  4:57 PM

		Check there is no ‘pending’ payout existing for this vendor. If there is, you should commit or cancel it.

You can check this in the UNIPaaS portal on the “Payouts List” page.
