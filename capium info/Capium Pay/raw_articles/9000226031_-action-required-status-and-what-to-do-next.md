### “Action Required” status and what to do next

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 6 Apr, 2023 at  8:15 AM

		There are required fields the vendor must complete for the onboarding. When the vendor is in this status open the onboarding link, it redirects them directly to the required field/s. UNIPaaS can send webhooks via API to the Capium Pay to update when the vendor changes status and indicate what data the vendor needs to input/update in their onboarding.
