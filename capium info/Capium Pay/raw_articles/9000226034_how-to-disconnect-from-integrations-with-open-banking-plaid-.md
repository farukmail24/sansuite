### How to disconnect from integrations with open banking &#39;Plaid&#39;?

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 6 Apr, 2023 at  8:15 AM

		Following GDPR, the vendor should convey his request directly to Plaid to the email address regulatory@plaid.com. To revoke Plaid access to the user’s bank account, the user should use his bank application and cancel the Open Banking/Plaid access permission (typically found under the "Security" or "Privacy" menus).
