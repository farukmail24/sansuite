### Why my card payment was declined?

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 6 Apr, 2023 at  8:15 AM

		The card issuer determines to approve or decline a card transaction based on their validations and checks. When a transaction is declined, the reason should be provided in the response. The decline reason should also be specified in the UNIPaaS portal on the “All Payments” page.
