### How to Include CT600L Supplementary Form

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 21 Oct, 2025 at 11:41 AM

If you need to include the CT600L supplementary form, you can easily do so by following these simple steps:

1. Navigate to Corporation Task: First, go to the Corporation Task section in your account.

2. Client Specific: Once in the Corporation Task section, select the Client Specific option.

3. CT600 Return: From there, click on CT600 Return.

4. Edit: Next, you can edit the CT 600 return, and then please navigate to the Main Form.

5. Page 2: Once in the Main Form, navigate to Page 2.

6. Check the Box 142: On Page 2, look for Box 142 and check it. This will prompt the addition of the CT600L form.

After following these steps, the CT600L form will be added to your return. If you encounter any issues or have any questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to our customer support team. We are here to help and ensure that you have a smooth experience using our platform.
