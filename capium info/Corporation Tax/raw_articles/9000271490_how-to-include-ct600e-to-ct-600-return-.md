### How to include CT600E to CT 600 return?

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 21 Oct, 2025 at 11:34 AM

		Title: How to Incorporate CT600E Return in Your Corporation Tax Filing

If you need to incorporate the CT600E supplementary form in your Corporation Tax filing, follow these simple steps:

1. Navigate to Corporation Tax: Access the Corporation Tax section in your account.

2. Client Specific: Select the client for whom you need to file the CT600E return.

3. Task: Click on the task option related to filing the Corporation Tax return.

4. CT600 Return: Choose the CT600 return option from the menu.

5. Edit: Click on the edit button to make changes to the main form.

6. Main Form: Navigate to the main form section and locate page 2.

7. Select Box 115: On page 2, select the checkbox for Box 115. This action will trigger the addition of a new CT600E form to your filing.

Incorporating the CT600E return is crucial if you have additional information or specific details that need to be included in your Corporation Tax filing. By following these steps, you can ensure that all necessary forms and information are accurately included in your return.

If you encounter any issues or have further questions about incorporating the CT600E return, please don't hesitate to reach out to our customer support team. We are here to assist you with any queries or concerns you may have regarding your Corporation Tax filing.
