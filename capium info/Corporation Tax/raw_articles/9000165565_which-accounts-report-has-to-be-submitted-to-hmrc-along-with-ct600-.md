### Which Accounts report has to be submitted to HMRC along with CT600?

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 25 Mar, 2019 at  1:17 PM

		You need to submit the Full set of accounts to the HMRC along with the CT600. 

You may submit the Abridged/Filleted Accounts to the Companies Houseseparately from the Capium Accounts Production Module.
