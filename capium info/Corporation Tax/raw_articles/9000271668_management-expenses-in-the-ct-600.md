### Management Expenses in the CT 600

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 22 Oct, 2025 at  8:58 AM

		Management Expenses in the CT 600

In the CT 600 form, there are specific areas where you need to manually enter the details for management expenses related to boxes 780 and 850. Here's how you can do it:

1. Navigate to the Corporation Tax module.

2. Click on Client Specific and then select Tasks.

3. From the tasks, choose CT 600 returns.

4. Click on Action and then choose Edit.

5. This will take you to the main form where you can enter the details for management expenses in boxes 780 and 850.

If you encounter any difficulties or have further questions regarding the management expenses in the CT 600 form, please do not hesitate to reach out to our customer support team. We are here to assist you and provide the necessary guidance to ensure a smooth and hassle-free process.
