### How can I show the tax credit claimed on software development (R&D)?

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 25 Mar, 2019 at  1:19 PM

		You need to select CT600 Full form instead of a short form for that you need to edit CT600 with the help of action drop-down box. 

Then choose the R&D or “Films Enhanced Expenditure” under Calculators to enter the R&D tax credit.
