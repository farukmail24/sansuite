### How do we run a CT600 for greater than 12 months?

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 25 Mar, 2019 at  1:27 PM

		If there is a longer period, the system will automatically split into two returns. First 12 months period will be in first return and the remaining period will fall into second return as per HMRC norms.
