### IR Mark Not Found

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 21 Oct, 2025 at  4:19 PM

		Title: IR Mark Not Found

If you are experiencing the issue of the 2022 – IR mark not found in the Capital Allowances section of the Corporation Tax module, it may be due to assets being updated with names containing special characters without appropriate spacing. To resolve this issue, we recommend the following steps:

1. Review and update the asset names: Ensure that any special characters in the asset names are correctly spaced. This will help to ensure that the return can be submitted successfully to HMRC.

2. Resubmit the return: After updating the asset names, resubmit the return to HMRC. This should resolve the issue and allow the return to be processed without any further problems.

If you have followed these steps and are still experiencing the issue of the 2022 – IR mark not found, please do not hesitate to reach out to our customer support team for further assistance. Our team is here to help and will work with you to resolve the issue and ensure that your return is submitted successfully.

We understand that dealing with tax returns and HMRC can be complex, and we are committed to providing the support you need to navigate these challenges. Thank you for choosing our services, and please let us know if there is anything else we can do to assist you.
