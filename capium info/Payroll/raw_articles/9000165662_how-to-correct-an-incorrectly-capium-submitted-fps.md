### How to correct an incorrectly Capium submitted FPS

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 22 Nov, 2021 at  4:40 PM

		Navigation:

Manage Payroll>> Process Payroll>> Payroll>> Delete from Action Dropdown

Help Guide:

If an incorrect FPS has been submitted to Capium, follow the navigation to delete the payroll that's been processed.

You can make the adjustments to the payroll and make the corrected FPS submission.
