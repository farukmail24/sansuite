### Smart Pension PAPDIS File

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 15 Sep, 2021 at  4:03 PM

		Location: 

Payroll Dashboard > Select a Client > Auto Enrolment > Pension Schemes > Contribution button

PAPDIS - short for the Payroll and Pension Data Interface Standard. It's free and open standard designed to help payroll systems to transfer information to pension providers.

Help Guide:

We have provided the option to download the Smart Pension Contribution file in the required PAPDIS format. 

To do this, head to the navigation above and once there, click Contribution, you will be presented with the below pop-up interface - simply click Download.
