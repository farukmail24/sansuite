### How to bulk download P11D reports

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 24 May, 2022 at  4:52 PM

		Location:

Select client from Payroll dashboard > Navigate into P11D > Select 'Bulk Report Download' > Choose to either generate the report for all employees or a selected few > Click on 'P11D PDF Download' 

Video Tutorial: 

Help Guide:

Navigate into the client portal and select 'P11D form'.

Click on the button 'Bulk Report Download'.

Select the year which the P11D submission was made and choose to generate the report for all employees or a selected few. 

Click on 'P11D PDF Download' to generate the report.
