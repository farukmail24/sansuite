### How can we change the Staging date after adding the pension scheme?

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 26 Mar, 2019 at  1:12 PM

		You need to delete the pension scheme to edit the staging date.

Please navigate to Auto Enrolment>>Pension Scheme to delete the scheme.
