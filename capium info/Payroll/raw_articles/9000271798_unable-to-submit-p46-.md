		Unable to Submit P46 

If you are having trouble submitting a P46, there are a few things to keep in mind. Here are some common issues and solutions to help you get back on track:

1. Check the Deadlines: Make sure you are aware of the deadlines for P46 submissions. The deadlines may vary depending on the specific circumstances, so it's important to stay informed and submit the necessary information on time.

2. Verify the Information: Double-check the information you are entering on the P46 form. Any inaccuracies or missing details could prevent the submission from going through. Take the time to review the form and ensure all the information is correct.
