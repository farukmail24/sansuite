### The employees do not appear in pension. Can you help?

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 26 Mar, 2019 at 11:30 AM

		The payroll has processed but you have not submitted the FPS to HMRC. 

Please submit the FPS and then the employees will appear in pension scheme contribution file and you can submit to your pension provider.
