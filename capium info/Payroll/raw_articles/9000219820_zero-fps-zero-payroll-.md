### Zero FPS (Zero Payroll)

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 4 Nov, 2022 at 12:14 AM

		Location: Payroll > Client Specific > Manage Payroll > Process Payroll > Payroll

 After you have navigated your way to the payroll, select 'Run Payroll'.

Select 'Run Payroll' and the following will appear. 

Tick the 'Zero Payslip' checkbox.

Once ticked, select next to display the 'Payroll Details' which will show no figures. 

Once you are happy with the figures, select finish to complete the payroll.
