### National Insurance information

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 6 Apr, 2023 at  8:15 AM

		To run payroll reports in Capium and ensure national insurance contributions have been met.

Help/Guide

Navigation: Payroll > Reports > Periodic Tax and NI 

To ensure that all tax and national insurance contributions are met, a user needs to follow navigation process and select a period of the report shown on the screenshot above.

Once it has been done, a user is able to see Employer’s payment record including tax and national insurance contributions. P.S. Capium calculates contributions automatically on a regular basis, based on the UK legislation rules.
