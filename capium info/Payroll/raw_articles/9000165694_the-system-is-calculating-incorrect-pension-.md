### The system is calculating incorrect pension.

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 26 Mar, 2019 at 12:25 PM

		It looks like you have updated the Lower Band Limit (£6,032.00) and Upper Band Limit (£46,350.00) while adding the pension scheme. 

Hence the system is calculating the pension after the band threshold limits.
