		How to Print/Download a P60 for Previous Years

If you need to print a P60 for the previous year, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to Payroll in your account.

2. Click on Client-specific.

3. Select Reports.

4. Choose Additional Forms.

5. Click on Form P60.

6. Select the appropriate pay frequency and year.

7. Choose the employee for whom you need to print/download the P60.

8. Click on the print/PDF icon.

If you encounter any difficulties while following these steps, please refer to the attached file for guidance. If you still need assistance, please reach out to our customer support team for further help.
