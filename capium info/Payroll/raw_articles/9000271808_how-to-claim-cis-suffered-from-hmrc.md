### How to Claim CIS Suffered from HMRC

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 22 Oct, 2025 at  4:33 PM

		Title: How to Claim CIS Suffered Due to HMRC

If you have suffered CIS (Construction Industry Scheme) deductions and need to claim them back from HMRC, you can easily do so through our payroll system. Here's how:

1. Navigate to the Payroll module.

2. Select the client for whom you need to make the CIS claim.

3. Go to the Submission tab and click on FPS & EPS.

4. Under EPS, select the tax year and click on Add Submission.

5. Enter the amount of CIS suffered that you are claiming back.

6. Click Submit to complete the process.

We hope this information helps you successfully claim back any CIS suffered due to HMRC. Thank you for choosing our services, and we are here to support you with any further questions or concerns.
