### Could you please suggest that how can I use the Net to Gross calculations in the payroll?

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 28 Apr, 2021 at 11:54 AM

		Use the Net to Gross Calculator from the Manage Payroll>> Process Payroll>> Record Gross Pay section.

Please refer below screenshot for more help.

Please note that the status needs to be pending of the client. 
 - Net to gross will work only for the employees who are on variable pay

Once a desired net pay is entered in the box, the gross pay will be calculated 

Once the save button is pressed the updated gross pay will be reflected in the payroll
