### How to submit a Period of Inactivity

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 17 Nov, 2021 at 11:53 AM

		Location:

Submissions > FPS & EPS > Click on the Employer Payment Summary > Add Submission

Video Tutorial:

Click here to watch how to submit a period of inactivity 

Help Guide:

There are two options for adding a period of inactivity:

"Period of Inactivity" - submit for the future period. 

"No Payment for the Period' - submit for the current period
