### Please suggest a direct method to calculate AWE for SSP

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 26 Mar, 2019 at 10:50 AM

		As of now, Capium is not calculating the AWE automatically and we have already forwarded this to the concerned department as a suggestion. You may calculate the AWE value as below.

AWE= Annual Gross Salary/52

 i.e. 52 is the total week number.
