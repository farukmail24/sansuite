### Process a leaver in Capium

			Print

	Modified on: Sat, 18 Oct, 2025 at  2:15 PM

To process a leaver in Capium, you can follow the steps below to ensure a smooth and accurate process:

1. Approve Gross Pay: Make sure to approve the gross pay for the employee for the relevant period before proceeding with the leaver process.

2. Pension Assessment: If the employee is enrolled in a pension scheme, approve the pension assessment. If there is no pension, you can skip this step.

3. Update Leaving Date: Navigate to Manage Payroll >> Employees and update the correct leaving date for the employee.

4. Run Payroll: Once the leaving date is updated, run the payroll for the period to ensure that the employee's final pay is calculated correctly.

5. Check Payslip: After running the payroll, check the employee's payslip to verify that all figures appear accurate and reflect the employee's final pay.

6. FPS Submission: If the payslip figures are correct, proceed with the Full Payment Submission (FPS) to HMRC.

7. P45 Form: Finally, check the P45 form under Additional Form >> Form P45 to ensure that it has been generated accurately for the leaver.

It's important to verify that the leaving date is updated in the record by downloading the HMRC report after the submission.

You can do this by navigating to submissions >> FPS & EPS >> Full Payment Submission(FPS) >> Select action >> Download HMRC Report.
