### Client User Access to Payroll

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 22 Jun, 2021 at  3:25 PM

Please click here to watch what a client can access in payroll when given permission

You can give your client users access to view payroll by going to My Admin> Users> Edit User> Modules and click on payroll.

Help/Guide: 

When the client logs in to their user account they will see a payroll summary for their company

When clicking on manage payroll they can see

employees (but not add)
add departments
add Mileage claims/expense claims
add timekeeping records for hourly paid employees
print payslips

They will be able to access the following reports

Payroll summary
Periodic tax
Mileage and expense claim reports

Departmental reports
