### I am trying to re-run payroll for this month as there is an additional employee

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 26 Mar, 2019 at  1:20 PM

Please follow below steps to rerun the payroll.

 - Please download the HMRC response for the submitted FPS and delete the payroll (refer attached).
 - You have to rerun the payroll for the employee with the late reason (refer attached).
 - You have to select the same pay date while running the payroll.
 - Submit the FPS to the HMRC.
