### How to submit the final EPS

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 30 Mar, 2023 at 10:03 AM

		To submit the final EPS within Payroll. 

Navigation: Payroll > Submission > FPS & EPS > Employer Payment Summary (EPS)

Following the navigation process, a user is able to submit the final EPS by enabling the ‘Final submission’ option.

Once performed, a user is able to submit the final EPS to HMRC by clicking the ‘HMRC Submit’ option.
