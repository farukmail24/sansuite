### How to do a year end EPS submission

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 23 Mar, 2022 at  9:56 AM

Navigation:

Payroll Module >> Select a Client >> Submissions >> FPS& EPS >> Employer Payment Summary >> Add Submission.

While submitting the final FPS for the end of the year please follow this link.

If you have already made an FPS submission without selecting the final submission box, you can follow the steps listed below to make a final payroll submission for the year.

Step 1:

To go on the EPS submission tab, alternatively click here to be redirected to the page. 

Step 2:

Click on add submission.

Step 3:

On the EPS submission page, find the final submission box as shown below. 

Update the Final Submission box as >> Yes 

Once you have verified all the other information you can proceed to submit to HMRC using the button highlighted in the red box.
