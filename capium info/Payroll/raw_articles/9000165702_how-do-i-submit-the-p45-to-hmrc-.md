### How do I submit the P45 to HMRC?

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 26 Mar, 2019 at 12:28 PM

		1. It is necessary to run at least a single payroll to notify the Leaver to the HMRC and generate the Form P45. (If there is no pay you may run zero payrolls)

2. Once done you may download the P45 by following the below link

Reports>> Additional Forms>> P45
