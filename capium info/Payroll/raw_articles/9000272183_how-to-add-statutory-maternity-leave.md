### How to add Statutory Maternity Leave

			Print

	Modified on: Mon, 3 Nov, 2025 at  5:43 PM

		How to Add Statutory Maternity Leave in Capium

Adding statutory maternity leave in Capium is a simple process that can be completed in just a few steps. Follow the instructions below to ensure that your employee's statutory maternity pay is accurately recorded in the system.

1. Navigate to Manage Payroll: To begin, log in to your Client's Capium account and navigate to the Manage Payroll section. 

2. Additional: Once you are in the Manage Payroll section, look for the Additional options tab. 

3. Choose Leave Type: In the Employee Leave section, you will be prompted to select the type of leave you want to add for the employee. Choose "Statutory Maternity Pay" from the list of available leave types.

By following these simple steps, you can easily add statutory maternity leave in Capium and ensure that your employee's entitlement to maternity pay is properly managed within the system. If you encounter any issues or need further assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to our customer support team for help.
