### How to download your client&#39;s PAYE details

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 14 Sep, 2021 at  2:28 PM

		Location:

Payroll Dashboard >> 'PAYE Clients' >> Download button

Help Guide:

As seen below, you can download a list of all PAYE clients. This will be downloaded as a CSV and will include the following:

1. Client ID2. Client Email ID3. Phone Number4. Company Type5. Client Name6. HMRC office number7. PAYE reference Number8. Accounts office Number9. Client Status – Active / Inactive
