### Can I bulk download payroll record and reports for more than one client?

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 6 Aug, 2021 at 11:09 AM

		Yes! Please navigate to Payroll > Dashboard

Payroll Records

Click on PAYE Clients
Check the tick box on the left hand side of the clients you want to download a report
Click on download
A CSV will download with the PAYE payroll data for all clients selected

Bulk Reports

Click on PAYE Clients
Check the tick box on the left hand side of the clients you want to download a report
Click on Export Report
A pop up will appear

Please select from the dropdown boxes

From here you can download as PDF or email the reports
