### Unable to amend tax code

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 14 Nov, 2025 at  3:04 PM

		We understand that navigating tax codes can sometimes be confusing, and we're here to help clarify any issues you may encounter. This article addresses a common situation regarding tax codes, specifically the significance of the letter "X" in your tax information.

Understanding the Letter "X" in Your Tax Code

What Does "X" Mean?

The letter "X" in your tax code indicates that the employee is operating on a Week1/Month1 basis.

Updating Your Tax Information

To ensure that your tax information is accurate, please follow these steps:

1. Navigate to Manage Payroll in your payroll system.

2. Click on Employees to view your employee list.

3. Select the employee whose tax information you wish to update.

4. Click on Select Action and then choose Tax Info to review and modify the tax code as necessary.

Please confirm on your side and inform us if you encounter any difficulties.
