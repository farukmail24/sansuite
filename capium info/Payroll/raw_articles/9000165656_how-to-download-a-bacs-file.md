### How to download a BACS file

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 17 Nov, 2021 at  9:59 AM

		Location:

Submissions > FPS & EPS > Full Payment Summary > BACS File 

Help Guide:

The BACS file can be downloaded after making the FPS submission to HMRC.

To download the file, click on 'BACS File'.
