### How can I submit the Employment Allowance to my client?

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 29 Apr, 2021 at 11:30 AM

### Navigation: Payroll> Submissions> FPS & EPS > Employment Payment Summary

Click here to watch how to claim Employment Allowance

You may submit an EPS for the Employment Allowance.

Help/Guide:

Firstly, click Add Submission on the right side

A new record will be created.

Please click yes or no from the claim employment allowance box.

When yes is clicked, 5 boxes for DemiStateAid appear. If none of these aid allowance apply please click Not Applicable

If DemiStateAid is chosen HMRC will decide the account to claim.

Please then follow the page and fill out the boxes where claims are needed. 

Click whether to include bank details and whether this is a final submission

Finally, click HMRC submit.
