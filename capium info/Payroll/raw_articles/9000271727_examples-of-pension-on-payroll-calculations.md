### Examples of Pension on Payroll- Calculations

			Print

	Modified on: Wed, 22 Oct, 2025 at  7:19 AM

		Kindly review the following examples of pension calculations regarding 'no tax relief' and 'relief at source' as processed by the system:
​

No Tax Relief

Employee Net Pay Salary: £1000Deducting lower band limit (£6240 per year, £520 monthly): £1000 - £520 = £480 Employee contribution: 480 * 5% = £24Employer contribution: 480 * 3% = £14.4

Relief at Source

Employee Net Pay Salary: £1000Deducting lower band limit (£6240 per year, £520 monthly): £1000 - £520 = £480Tax Relief at Source: £480 * 20% = £96Employee Contribution: £384 * 5% = £19.2Employer Contribution: £384 * 3% = £11.5

Please review the calculations and inform us if you require any additional assistance.
