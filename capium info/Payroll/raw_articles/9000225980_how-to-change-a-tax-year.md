### How to change a Tax Year

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 30 Mar, 2023 at 10:04 AM

		To change a Tax Year once the current year’s final FPS and EPS are submitted. 

Navigation: Payroll > Selected client > General Settings > Change Tax Year

Following the navigation process, a user is able to change a Tax Year once the final FPS and EPS are submitted.
