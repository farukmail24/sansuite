### Client information - Creating sales and purchase invoices

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 23 Jul, 2021 at 11:46 AM

		Location:

Sales invoice 

Hub module >> Sales >> Invoice >> Raise invoice 

Purchase invoice

Hub module >> Purchases >> Purchases >> Record purchase 

Video Tutorial:

Click here to watch how to create sales and purchase invoices 

Help Guide:

Your clients can raise sales and purchase invoices from their side by logging in and going into the Hub module.

Sales Invoice

Navigate to 'Sales' and then 'Invoices'.

To create a new sales invoice click on 'Raise Invoice'.

Enter in the details of the invoice and click on 'Save'.

Purchase Invoice 

Navigate to 'Purchases' and go into the 'Purchases' tab.

To create a new invoice, click on 'Record Purchases'. 

Enter in the details of the invoice and click on 'Save'.

Once your clients have created the invoices, they should reflect in the bookkeeping module.
