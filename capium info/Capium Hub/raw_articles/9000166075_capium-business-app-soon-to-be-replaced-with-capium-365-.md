### Capium Business App (Soon to be replaced with Capium 365)

			Print

	Modified on: Fri, 13 Jun, 2025 at  3:28 PM

		Capium's Business App has been replaced with Capium 365. We will soon deprecate this mobile app and fully replace this with Capium 365. More on Capium 365 can be found here https://www.capium.com/products/capium365/

Here is also an article on how to get setup 

You can book a demo through www.capium.com and a member of the team will navigate you through how it's your solution for digital recordkeeping and MTD

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Capium's Business App helps accountants get data quicker from their clients in order to continue with filing preparations. 

It helps clients create invoices on the go, whilst also capturing receipts in real-time straight back to the accountant. It helps in line with Making Tax Digital (MTD) to speed up receiving transactions  

The Capium Business App  also houses DocScan, a tool designed to scan physical receipts, pick up the values and send to the accountant in real-time. Using the Bulk Upload feature, you are also able to take photos of up to 10 receipts or choose 10 photos from the gallery to upload to the Accountant. 

The app can be downloaded from the Apple Appstore and Google Playstore - just search Capium Business App
