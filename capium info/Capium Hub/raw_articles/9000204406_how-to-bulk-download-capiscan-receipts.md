### How to bulk download Docscan receipts

			Print

	Modified on: Tue, 13 Jul, 2021 at  3:04 PM

		Location:

Hub >> Select customer >> Purchases >> Docscan >> Filter dates to find the receipts >> Click on 'Export As' and select the format of the download

Video Tutorial:

Click here to watch how to bulk download Docscan receipts

Help Guide:

Navigate to the Hub module from the ecosystem, 

Select the customer for which the receipts need to be downloaded.

Click on purchases >> Docscan 

Enter a 'From' and 'To' date to filter the scanned receipts 

Click on the 'Export As' dropdown box to select the format you want to download the receipts
