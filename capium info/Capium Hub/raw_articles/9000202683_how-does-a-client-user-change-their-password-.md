### How does a Client User change their password?

			Print

	Modified on: Thu, 27 May, 2021 at 10:36 AM

		Please click here to watch how to change your password as a client user

Location: My Admin > Profile > Profile Settings 

To change your password whilst already logged into to your account, head to My Admin

This is the person icon in the top right hand corner

You will then enter into an admin portal

Please click on profile on the left hand side, please then click on change password

Please type in the original password given to you by your accountant

Please type in a new password, confirm the password and click save.
